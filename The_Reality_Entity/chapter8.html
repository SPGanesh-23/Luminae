✦ Chapter 8 ✦

After the Noise

✦

The classroom door slid open quietly.

But the moment Alex walked in, the entire room went silent. Every conversation stopped. Every chair movement froze. Even the teacher paused mid-sentence.

Blood stained Alex’s hands. Not fresh enough to drip anymore. Just dark red across his knuckles and fingers.

His shirt sleeve was torn slightly near the wrist, and there was a faint bruise forming near his jaw, but his face carried nothing.

No anger. No fear. Nothing.

He walked past everyone like he couldn’t even hear them.

Ryan stood up immediately.

“Alex—”

No response.

Ryan quickly moved toward him, concern written all over his face. “Dude, what the hell happened?”

Alex ignored him completely.

He reached his seat near the window and sat down slowly, resting his elbow against the desk while staring outside. The cloudy sky reflected faintly in his eyes.

Empty.

A few seconds later, Ethan entered the classroom. Lara walked beside him quietly.

The moment Ryan saw them, he turned sharply. “What happened there?”

Ethan exhaled heavily before dropping into the seat beside Ryan.

“…those guys crossed the line.”

Ryan frowned. “What did they do?”

For a moment, Ethan didn’t answer.

Lara glanced toward Alex before speaking softly. “They were harassing a girl near the back building.”

Ryan’s expression darkened instantly. “And Alex saw it?”

Ethan gave a small nod. “He tried to stop it normally at first.”

Ryan blinked. “Normally?”

“That lasted about ten seconds,” Ethan muttered.

Lara looked down slightly. “One of them grabbed her again even after Alex warned them.”

Silence.

Ryan slowly looked toward Alex.

Alex still hadn’t moved. Still staring outside.

Ethan leaned back in his chair. “I’ve never seen him that angry before.”

Ryan rubbed the back of his neck.

“…you should’ve gotten there sooner.”

Ethan’s jaw tightened slightly.

Ryan sighed. “You should’ve saved the girl earlier.”

A pause.

“You were late. That’s why Alex lost it.”

The words hit harder than anyone expected.

Alex froze. Completely.

For a split second—

Rain slammed against concrete. Red and blue lights flashed violently through darkness. Cold fingers slipping from his grip. A girl crying.

Someone screaming directly into his face—

“You were supposed to protect her!”

“You were late!”

A sharp ringing exploded inside his ears.

Then—

The classroom returned.

Noise. Breathing. Voices.

Alex’s fingers clenched slowly beneath the desk.

“…shut up.”

The words came out low.

Dangerously low.

Ryan immediately went silent.

Even Ethan noticed it.

Something about Alex’s expression had changed for just a second.

Not rage.

Something worse.

Pain.

But before anyone could say anything else, the teacher cleared her throat awkwardly near the board.

“Alright everyone… settle down.”

The tension in the room slowly broke apart.

Classes continued, though nobody really focused anymore. Whispers occasionally spread across the room. Students kept glancing toward Alex.

Some nervous. Some curious. Some scared.

Meanwhile, Alex remained near the window silently the entire time.

The teacher eventually looked toward Lara.

“And since today is your first official day here… would you like to introduce yourself?”

Lara blinked slightly before standing up. The class slowly shifted their attention toward her.

“My name is Lara,” she said softly. “I transferred here recently… I hope we get along.”

A few students greeted her casually. Some boys immediately straightened in their seats.

Ryan smirked slightly.

Ethan rolled his eyes.

Alex didn’t react at all.

But Lara noticed something.

Even while staring outside—

Alex was listening.

Time passed slowly.

Eventually—

The lunch bell rang.

The classroom instantly exploded into noise. Students rushed outside toward the cafeteria within seconds.

Ryan stood up immediately.

“Finally. I’m starving.”

Ethan grabbed his wallet from his pocket. “Come before all the good food disappears.”

Ryan looked toward Alex.

“You coming?”

“No.”

Typical.

Ryan sighed dramatically.

“Bro survives fights but loses against social interaction.”

Ethan snorted quietly. “Come on.”

The two eventually left the classroom.

Soon—

Only Alex and Lara remained.

The room became quieter again. Soft wind drifted through the slightly opened window.

For a while, neither of them spoke.

Alex still stared outside silently, his thoughts somewhere far away.

Then Lara quietly walked toward the desk beside him and sat down.

“You should probably wash your hands.”

Alex glanced down briefly at the dried blood across his knuckles.

“…probably.”

“But you won’t.”

“…probably.”

A tiny smile appeared on Lara’s face.

For the first time that day—

Alex’s expression softened slightly.

Very slightly.

Lara noticed it immediately.

Her eyes slowly drifted toward his hand again.

Up close, the damage looked worse. Bruised knuckles. Small cuts. Dried blood across his fingers.

Without thinking much, she gently reached for his wrist.

Alex immediately tensed.

The reaction was instant. Like his body moved before his mind could.

But Lara didn’t let go immediately.

“…your hand’s injured.”

“It’s fine.”

“You punched someone hard enough to leave blood on your sleeve.”

“…still fine.”

“You’re a terrible liar.”

Alex looked at her for a second.

Then quietly looked away again.

Lara carefully loosened her grip and leaned back slightly.

“You scared everyone today,” she said softly.

Alex stared out the window again.

“They deserved worse.”

No hesitation. No guilt. Just certainty.

Lara studied him silently for a moment.

“But you still stopped after Ethan pulled you away.”

Alex didn’t answer.

A quiet breeze passed through the classroom.

Then Lara spoke again.

“I know everyone’s scared of you now…”

Alex’s eyes shifted slightly toward her.

“…but I wasn’t.”

That caught him off guard.

For the first time since entering the classroom—

Alex actually looked at her properly.

“…why?”

Lara shrugged lightly.

“Because bad people don’t protect strangers.”

Silence.

Alex looked down at his injured hand again.

After a while, he spoke quietly.

“People like that don’t stop unless someone makes them stop.”

The way he said it felt wrong. Too experienced. Too personal.

Lara noticed it immediately. Like this wasn’t the first time Alex had seen something like that.

For a moment, neither of them spoke.

Then Lara suddenly tilted her head slightly.

“You know…”

Alex looked at her.

“You’re less scary when you’re quiet.”

“…what?”

“And honestly?”

A small grin appeared on her face.

“You look emotionally exhausted.”

Alex stared at her blankly.

“…that’s somehow more insulting.”

Lara laughed softly.

A real laugh. Not forced. Not awkward.

And somehow—

That sound alone eased something inside the room.

Alex blinked slightly. Then—

For the first time—

A small laugh escaped him too.

Tiny. Barely there. But real.

Lara immediately noticed. Her eyes widened slightly.

“Oh.”

Alex instantly looked away again.

“…don’t make it weird,” Alex muttered quietly.

“I just discovered something historic.” Lara said with an amused grin.

“It wasn’t that serious,” he replied flatly.

“You smiled.” Lara pointed out immediately.

“I didn’t,” Alex denied without hesitation.

“You literally did.” She laughed softly.

“I absolutely did not,” he argued while looking away again.

Lara laughed again. And this time—

Alex didn’t look away immediately.

The classroom door suddenly burst open.

Ryan walked in carrying multiple food trays while Ethan followed behind him.

“There they are.”

Ryan immediately narrowed his eyes dramatically. “Aha.”

Ethan already looked exhausted. “Don’t start.”

Ryan ignored him completely.

Slowly, he looked between Alex and Lara. Then grinned. “Soooo…”

Alex sighed instantly.

Ryan pointed accusingly toward Alex. “This is the special girl you walked home with, that night ah?”

Lara nearly choked. “What?!”

Ethan immediately started laughing.

Alex looked genuinely irritated now. “Ryan!!”

“OHHHH so it IS true.”, Ryan said loudly, pointing at him dramatically.

“It’s not”, Alex replied immediately with visible irritation.

Ryan placed the food trays down while grinning like an idiot.

“Bro was ready to kill people this morning and now suddenly he’s peacefully sitting here during lunch with one girl.”, Ryan said with a smug grin.

Lara’s cheeks turned slightly red.

Alex looked away toward the window immediately. Which honestly answered the question even more.

Ryan slapped the desk dramatically. “I KNEW IT.”

“Shut up” Alex muttered while looking away toward the window.

Ethan sat beside Ryan while laughing quietly. “You’re making it worse.”

“This is cinema,” Ryan declared proudly.

For the first time since the incident—

Alex almost smiled.

Almost.

And for a little while…

Everything felt normal again.

The laughter. The teasing. The warmth inside the classroom.

It felt distant from violence. Distant from fear. Distant from blood.

But far beyond the school walls—

Something watched silently.

Not from the sky.

Not from the darkness beyond reality.

But from directly behind the violet-tinted lenses resting quietly on Alex’s face.

Waiting. Observing.

Deep within the distortion space hidden inside the glasses, countless fractured screens floated endlessly around the entity.

One screen showed Alex.

Another showed Lara.

Then—

For the first time in a long time—

The entity noticed it.

An emotional connection.

A weakness.

And slowly…

The distorted screens around it began to flicker.
