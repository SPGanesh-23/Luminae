✦ Chapter 15 ✦

The East Wing

✦

The school corridors had grown almost completely silent by the time the four of them reached the eastern wing.

Even the sunset outside looked dimmer here.

Orange light stretched weakly across the old hallway floors while several ceiling lights flickered lazily overhead.

Ryan walked slightly behind everyone with both hands inside his pockets.

“I’m telling you right now,” he declared dramatically, “if one ghost child runs across this hallway, I’m sacrificing Ethan and leaving.”

Ethan looked exhausted already.

“Why am I always first?”

“Because you run the slowest.”

“I literally play basketball.”

“Emotionally slow then.”

Lara laughed quietly.

Alex kept walking ahead silently.

Then Ethan suddenly slowed near the lockers lining the wall.

“…wait.”

Everyone looked toward him.

Dark reddish stains still stretched faintly across several dented lockers near the corner of the hallway.

The metal remained slightly bent inward from the earlier fight.

Ryan stared for a second.

“…damn.”

Even Lara grew quieter.

The atmosphere shifted immediately.

Because suddenly—

the fight from earlier no longer felt distant.

It happened here.

Right here.

Ryan slowly looked toward Alex.

“You really turned this hallway into a UFC event.”

Alex glanced toward the damaged lockers briefly.

“They started it.”

“That sentence is carrying this entire story,” Ethan muttered.

Ryan walked closer toward one of the dents in the metal locker and whistled softly.

“Bro got jumped by six people and STILL redecorated the building.”

Alex ignored him completely.

A few moments later—

they finally arrived outside the old library.

The large wooden doors stood partially open now after the principal unlocked them earlier.

Dust floated through the dim evening light leaking from inside.

The place somehow looked even older now.

Ryan immediately pointed toward the entrance.

“Yep. Haunted.”

“It’s literally books,” Lara replied.

“Haunted books.”

Alex quietly stepped inside first.

The others followed behind him.

The smell of old paper and dust filled the air instantly.

Tall shelves stretched endlessly into the dim interior while white cloth covers rested over abandoned furniture near the back sections.

The library was massive.

Far bigger than any of them expected.

Ryan blinked slowly.

“Okay why does this place look like it belongs in a horror game?”

“Because nobody’s cleaned it in years,” Ethan answered.

Alex silently grabbed several cleaning supplies stacked near the entrance wall.

Buckets.

Cloths.

Boxes.

Then without hesitation—

he started assigning work calmly.

“Ethan, left shelves.”

Ethan caught the cleaning cloth thrown toward him.

“Alright.”

“Ryan, clear the reading tables.”

Ryan looked offended immediately.

“Why do I sound like unpaid labor?”

“You are unpaid labor.”

“Tragic.”

Alex handed another cloth toward Lara.

“You take the center shelves.”

Then Ryan narrowed his eyes slowly.

“…wait.”

Alex looked at him.

“What are YOU going to do?”

Alex answered casually,

“I’m helping Lara.”

Ryan froze dramatically.

Then slowly looked between both of them.

Then pointed accusingly.

“OHHHH.”

Lara immediately knew where this was going.

“No.”

Ryan ignored her completely.

“Now I understand.”

Ethan sighed softly.

“Please don’t.”

Ryan placed one hand against his chest dramatically.

“Couple goals inside haunted libraries. Cinema.”

“It’s not like that,” Lara replied instantly.

Ryan looked unconvinced.

“Bro nearly died protecting her TWICE today.”

Alex looked genuinely confused now.

“That feels unrelated.”

Ryan stared at him in disbelief.

“You are emotionally blind.”

“Clinically blind without glasses too,” Ethan added.

Ryan slapped Ethan’s shoulder proudly.

“SEE? He gets me.”

Alex sighed quietly and walked deeper into the library beside Lara while Ryan continued complaining dramatically in the background.

Eventually—

the huge shelves naturally separated everyone further apart.

The library became quieter.

Distant cleaning sounds echoed faintly from the other sections while dust floated lazily through beams of orange evening light above them.

Lara dusted several books carefully before glancing sideways toward Alex beside her.

He was reorganizing old books unusually fast.

Almost automatically.

Like he already knew where things belonged.

Then her eyes slowly drifted toward his glasses.

“…Alex?”

“Hm?”

“Can I ask something weird?”

“That usually means danger.”

Lara ignored the comment.

“Your glasses…”

Alex looked toward her slightly.

“What about them?”

She hesitated briefly.

Then spoke quieter.

“…is there something wrong with them?”

For the first time—

Alex paused.

Only slightly.

“…why?”

Lara lowered the cloth in her hand slowly.

“Earlier… during the fight…”

Her expression tightened faintly.

“I felt something.”

Silence.

She looked directly toward him now.

“When I picked up your glasses…”

Alex stayed completely still.

“…it felt like something was inside them.”

The air suddenly felt colder.

Lara looked unsure of her own words now.

“I know that sounds insane,” she admitted quietly. “But for a second it looked like something was alive inside the lenses.”

Alex’s expression became unreadable again.

Not angry.

Not shocked.

Just still.

Then softly—

“What exactly did you see?”

Before Lara could answer—

CRASH.

A massive sound exploded from the far end of the library.

Both of them immediately turned.

Ryan’s voice echoed seconds later.

“THAT WASN’T MY FAULT.”

Alex instantly sighed.

“…unbelievable.”

Lara nearly laughed.

Alex pointed lightly toward the shelves.

“Keep working.”

“And you?”

“I’m going to stop Ryan from destroying the building.”

“That might actually be impossible.”

“Probably.”

Alex walked toward the sound while Lara returned to dusting the shelves quietly.

Meanwhile—

near the far reading section—

an entire bookshelf had collapsed sideways onto the floor while books scattered everywhere.

Ryan stood frozen beside it holding one random encyclopedia.

Ethan looked seconds away from violence.

“How,” he asked slowly, “did you even manage this?”

Ryan pointed toward the shelf defensively.

“It attacked first.”

Alex arrived a second later and immediately stopped walking.

“…Ryan.”

Ryan raised both hands instantly.

“I can explain.”

“I genuinely don’t think you can.”

“It was structurally emotional.”

“That is not a sentence.”

Ethan rubbed his forehead.

“I told him not to climb it.”

Alex blinked slowly.

“…you climbed the bookshelf?”

Ryan pointed toward the upper shelves.

“How else was I supposed to reach the top?”

“There are ladders.”

“Oh.”

Alex looked physically exhausted now.

Then suddenly—

something caught his attention.

A faint cracking sound.

Tiny.

Sharp.

His eyes shifted upward instantly.

Far across the library—

directly above Lara—

a large crack stretched across the old ceiling.

Dust drifted downward slowly.

The structure was giving way.

Alex’s expression changed immediately.

“Lara!”

No response.

She stood near the center shelves quietly organizing books—

AirPods still in.

Didn’t hear him.

The crack spread wider.

FAST.

“LARA!”

Still nothing.

Ryan and Ethan turned immediately.

Alex grabbed a nearby book and threw it hard across the library.

It slammed against the shelf beside her loudly.

Lara flinched slightly—

but didn’t look up.

The ceiling above her split violently.

Chunks of concrete started falling.

Too fast.

Too far.

Alex understood instantly.

He couldn’t reach her in time.

For a single terrifying second—

everything slowed.

Lara beneath the collapsing ceiling.

Ryan shouting something.

Ethan running forward.

Dust exploding downward.

And Alex—

standing frozen several meters away.

Then slowly—

he closed his eyes.

And somewhere deep inside him—

a desperate thought formed.

A wish.

Not logical.

Not possible.

Just one overwhelming need.

Please.

Save her.

The world suddenly distorted.

Somewhere beyond reality—

the entity awakened instantly.

Usually—it rewrote probability.

Small changes.

Logical outcomes.

But this?

There was no logical way to save her now.

For the first time—

the entity forced reality itself to break.

Directly above Lara—

space cracked open.

A massive black fracture ripped silently through the air itself like shattered glass across reality.

And the collapsing ceiling vanished into it.

Gone.

Completely.

Silence.

The crack in space closed instantly afterward.

Dust drifted softly through the library.

Nothing else moved.

Ryan stood frozen.

Ethan stared in complete disbelief.

Lara slowly removed one AirPod.

“…what just happened?”

Nobody answered.

Because near the center of the library—

reality itself had just broken.
