✦ Chapter 14 ✦

The Library That Shouldn’t Breathe

✦

A sharp ringing echoed faintly inside Alex’s ears.

Then slowly—

consciousness returned.

The first thing he noticed was the ceiling light above him.

White.

Too bright.

The second thing he noticed was the smell of antiseptic.

School clinic.

Alex blinked slowly before trying to sit up slightly.

Pain immediately pulled through his ribs.

“…ow.”

His voice came out rough.

The nurse sitting near the desk glanced over instantly.

“Well look who finally decided to wake up.”

Alex ignored the comment completely.

His eyes immediately searched the room.

Left.

Right.

The nearby chair.

The doorway.

“…where’s Lara?”

The nurse paused for a second.

Then a tiny smile appeared on her face.

“Good morning to you too.”

Before Alex could respond—

the clinic door opened.

Lara stepped inside carrying a small paper bag filled with medicines and bottled water.

The moment Alex saw her—

something inside him relaxed slightly.

Not visibly.

But enough.

Behind her, the principal stood outside the clinic speaking quietly with the doctor near the hallway.

Lara noticed Alex sitting up immediately.

“You’re awake.”

“You disappeared.”

“I went to get your medicines. You were unconscious for twenty minutes.”

“…oh.”

The nurse sighed dramatically while standing up.

“He’s fine. Unfortunately.”

Alex looked mildly offended.

The nurse walked over toward Lara and handed her two tablets.

“Give him these for now.”

Then she pointed toward Alex.

“And make sure he takes them instead of pretending he’s immortal.”

“No promises,” Alex muttered.

“I heard that.”

Lara laughed softly while the nurse left the room.

For a moment—

the clinic became quieter.

Evening sunlight filtered softly through the curtains while distant school noises echoed faintly outside.

Lara walked over and sat beside the bed before handing him the water bottle.

“Take these.”

Alex stared at the tablets suspiciously.

“…what are they?”

“Medicine.”

“That sounds untrustworthy.”

“You literally got punched through lockers today.”

“And survived.”

“Barely.”

Alex still looked unconvinced.

Lara narrowed her eyes slightly.

“…Alex.”

“…fine.”

He finally took the tablets with visible reluctance before leaning back against the pillow again.

Silence settled briefly.

Then Lara quietly looked toward the bandage wrapped around his knuckles.

“You scared me today.”

Alex looked away toward the window immediately.

“They weren’t supposed to involve you.”

“That’s not what I meant.”

A pause.

Lara exhaled softly.

“When they surrounded you…”

Her voice lowered slightly.

“…you still stood back up.”

Alex stayed quiet.

“I thought you were seriously hurt.”

“I’m okay.”

“You were bleeding.”

“I do that sometimes apparently.”

Lara laughed despite herself.

“You’re impossible.”

“I’m POSSIBLE.”

Another small silence followed.

Comfortable this time.

Then Lara suddenly tilted her head slightly.

“You know something?”

“What?”

“You looked really stupid unconscious.”

Alex blinked slowly.

“…excuse me?”

“You were drooling a little too.”

“I absolutely was not.”

“You absolutely were.”

“That feels medically inaccurate.”

Lara grinned now.

“You also grabbed my sleeve while unconscious.”

Alex froze.

“…what?”

“You heard me.”

“I did not do that.”

“You literally refused to let go for five minutes.”

Alex immediately looked away.

“…fake news.”

Lara laughed quietly again.

The sound felt warm against the quiet clinic room.

Then she noticed something near his cheek.

“…wait.”

Alex looked back.

“There’s still blood here.”

Before he could react—

Lara leaned slightly closer and gently wiped the corner of his lip using tissue from the bedside table.

Alex immediately went still.

Completely still.

Lara noticed instantly.

“…why do you look terrified?”

“I’m evaluating the situation.”

“You fought six people today.”

“Yes.”

“But THIS is stressful?”

“…significantly.”

Lara nearly laughed again.

Then suddenly—

BANG.

The clinic door burst open violently.

“IS HE DEAD?!”

Ryan’s voice echoed across the entire room.

Alex visibly flinched.

Ethan walked in behind him looking exhausted already.

“For the sake of god stop screaming.”

Ryan ignored him completely and pointed dramatically toward Alex lying on the bed.

“Oh my god.”

Alex sighed instantly.

“You survived,” Ryan whispered emotionally.

“Unfortunately,” Ethan added.

Ryan grabbed a nearby chair and sat down backwards dramatically.

“Bro I leave school for ONE HOUR and suddenly you’re starring in Tokyo Revengers.”

Alex looked tired already.

“It wasn’t that serious.”

“YOU GOT JUMPED BY SIX PEOPLE.”

“Approximately.”

Ryan looked horrified.

“HE EVEN SAYS IT CALMLY.”

Ethan crossed his arms near the wall.

“Who were they?”

“Seniors,” Lara answered quietly.

“The same idiots from before?”

Alex nodded once.

Ryan pointed accusingly toward Alex again.

“And you still fought all of them?”

Alex thought for a second.

“…not ideally.”

“That is NOT reassuring.”

Lara shook her head softly.

“He almost got destroyed at first.”

Ryan slowly turned toward Alex with betrayal in his eyes.

“You LOST?”

“There were variables.”

“What does that even MEAN?”

“It means six people is statistically inconvenient,” Ethan replied dryly.

Ryan slapped the armrest dramatically.

Then his expression suddenly changed.

“…wait.”

His eyes slowly moved between Alex and Lara.

Then narrowed.

“Why does this room feel weirdly romantic?”

Alex looked genuinely offended now.

“It’s literally a clinic.”

“Exactly. Peak romance cinema.”

Lara nearly choked laughing.

Ethan rubbed his forehead.

“You’re mentally exhausting.”

Ryan pointed toward Alex proudly.

“And yet somehow I’m still healthier than him.”

“Debatable,” Ethan muttered.

The clinic door opened again before Ryan could continue speaking.

The nurse stepped back inside holding several papers.

“Well,” she said casually while looking over Alex’s condition, “you’re perfectly alright.”

Ryan looked shocked.

“That sentence feels illegal.”

The nurse ignored him completely.

“You didn’t receive any major hits.”

Then she glanced toward Alex’s bruised knuckles.

“In fact, I think the other students should be more worried than you.”

Lara tried not to smile.

Even Ethan looked mildly impressed.

The nurse handed the discharge papers toward Alex.

“You’re good to go.”

Ryan immediately stood up dramatically.

“HE LIVES.”

“Please calm down,” Ethan said.

A few minutes later—

everyone slowly stepped outside the clinic together.

Ryan kept talking nonsense.

Ethan kept correcting him.

Lara walked quietly beside Alex.

Then the nurse suddenly called out from behind.

“Alex.”

He turned back slightly.

The others kept walking ahead.

The nurse stood quietly near the clinic doorway now.

Her expression softer than before.

Then she glanced briefly toward Lara further ahead in the hallway.

And looked back at him.

“Always stay by her side.”

Alex stayed silent.

“She was the one who stayed beside you the entire time while you were unconscious.”

His eyes shifted slightly.

The nurse continued quietly,

“She cried for you.”

Alex froze for a second.

“She cares about you deeply.”

The hallway suddenly felt quieter.

Then the nurse smiled faintly.

“So protect her with your life.”

A pause.

“And don’t lose her at any cost.”

For once—

Alex didn’t know what to say.

So he simply nodded once but he understood how much Lara cared for him.

Then quietly walked away.

Outside the clinic, the principal still stood near the hallway windows speaking with the doctor.

When he noticed Alex approaching, he sighed softly.

“You should all head home and rest.”

Alex adjusted his bag over his shoulder calmly.

“We’ll check the library first.”

The principal blinked.

“…today?”

“We need to make it functional within a month.”

His voice remained calm.

“We can’t waste days.”

The principal looked genuinely surprised for a second.

Then before he could answer—

Ryan suddenly stepped between them dramatically.

“Correction.”

Everyone looked at him.

Ryan pointed toward himself proudly.

“We are checking the library.”

Ethan sighed instantly.

“Why are you involving me?”

“Because horror movies require side characters.”

“You just volunteered us to die.”

Ryan ignored him completely and pointed toward Lara too.

“And obviously we can’t let these two investigate haunted buildings alone.”

“It’s not haunted,” Lara replied.

Ryan looked at the dark eastern hallway in the distance.

“…that building absolutely eats people.”

Alex almost smiled slightly.

Almost.

Then together—

the four of them slowly began walking toward the eastern wing while evening sunlight stretched long shadows across the empty school corridors.

And somewhere far ahead—

the old library waited silently.
