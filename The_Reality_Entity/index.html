The Cast

Voices Across the Membrane

Many souls. Two timelines. One fracture in reality.

Alex
Protagonist · Timeline 2026
The Host
A quiet, observant student who intervenes in a fight without thinking twice. Unknowingly the carrier of the Reality Entity, his small acts of concern become cosmic turning points.
Kyros
Protagonist · Timeline Unknown
The Anomaly
Thrown into a broken timeline from the battle of 5026. He moves through temporal distortions without a watch—something that should be impossible. Solution or cause? No one knows yet.
The Reality Entity
Entity
Consciousness
A mind born from science beyond comprehension, now hiding inside a pair of scratched lenses. It watches. It learns. It edits. And it is only beginning to understand what it means to feel something.
Iris
Female Lead · Timeline Unknown
Precision Fighter
The woman who knocked Kyros out with a single calculated strike. Sharp, controlled, and exact. She leads with logic, not aggression—and is the first to treat Kyros as something other than a threat.
Lara
Female Lead · Timeline 2026
Principal's Daughter
Quiet, perceptive, and self-possessed beyond her years. She sees what others miss—not because she looks harder, but because she listens. Her calm presence becomes an unexpected anchor in Alex's world.
Ethan
The Enforcer · Timeline 2026
The Enforcer
The school's unofficial keeper of order. Seniors listen. Juniors fear him. His suspension created a power vacuum that lasted exactly one chapter. The first person the Entity brought back.
Cain Orel
The Leader · Timeline Unknown
The Leader
Scarred and steady. Leadership without effort. He runs the bunker crew with controlled precision, adapts to what the distortion demands, and watches Kyros with the calm of someone who's seen too many variables go wrong.
Ron
The Survivor · Timeline Unknown
The Survivor
First to confront Kyros in the fractured city. Bandaged, bruised, and unyielding. He doesn't trust easily—and he doesn't pretend to. His instinct is survival, not diplomacy, and he makes that clear.
