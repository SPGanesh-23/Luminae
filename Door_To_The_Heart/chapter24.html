✦ Chapter Twenty-Four ✦

“You Won’t Leave Me … Right?”

✦

The apartment was dark and quiet.

Only the faint light from outside entered through the curtains while Zayn slowly opened his eyes.

For a few seconds—

he looked confused.

Then he noticed the empty couch.

And immediately sat up.

“Luna?”

No answer.

Zayn quickly stood and walked toward the balcony door slightly left open.

Cold night wind entered softly.

And there she was.

Luna stood alone in the balcony hugging herself quietly.

Her eyes looked distant.

Lost somewhere far away.

Zayn’s expression softened immediately.

He slowly walked toward her.

“Hey.”

Luna quickly wiped her face before turning slightly.

“You’re awake?”

“You disappeared.”

“I couldn’t sleep.”

Zayn stayed quiet for a moment before gently placing his jacket around her shoulders.

“You’ll get sick standing here.”

Luna gave a tiny smile.

But it disappeared quickly too.

And somehow—

that scared Zayn more.

Because Luna always tried to smile through pain.

Even now.

After a few minutes, Zayn finally sighed softly.

“Come inside.”

Luna nodded quietly.

A little later—

Zayn stood near the apartment door getting ready to leave.

But before walking out—

he looked back at Luna again.

Still sitting silently.

Still thinking too much.

That uneasy feeling inside him grew stronger.

Finally—

“Luna.”

She looked up slowly.

“What happened today?”

Luna immediately looked away.

“Nothing.”

“Don’t do that.”

His voice softened.

“You only go quiet like this when something hurts.”

Silence.

Then slowly—

Luna stood up and walked toward him.

Zayn stayed patient.

Waiting.

And finally—

in a very small voice—

“I saw Kyros today.”

Zayn froze slightly.

“…Where?”

Luna swallowed hard.

“At work.”

Her eyes slowly filled again.

“He’s the owner of the company.”

The words broke halfway.

And suddenly—

tears escaped before she could stop them.

“I didn’t know…”

Her breathing became uneven immediately.

“I didn’t know it was HIS company…”

Zayn’s face softened instantly seeing her cry like this.

“Hey hey…”

He quickly pulled her closer carefully.

“It’s okay.”

But Luna shook her head immediately.

“No it’s not…”

Her fingers tightened against his shirt.

“The way he looked at me today…”

Her voice cracked painfully.

“…it hurt.”

Zayn closed his eyes briefly.

Because deep inside—

he already knew this would happen eventually.

No matter how much time passed…

Kyros still affected her.

Maybe always would.

Luna suddenly looked up at him again with trembling eyes.

Luna’s eyes searched his desperately.

And quietly—

“Zayn…”

“Hm?”

Before Luna could speak again—

Zayn suddenly looked away for a second.

Like he was fighting something inside his head.

Then finally—

in a low voice—

“After meeting Kyros again…”

Luna froze slightly.

Zayn forced out a tiny smile that looked painfully weak.

“…you won’t leave me right?”

The question hit her immediately.

Because this was the first time—

Zayn sounded scared.

Not calm.

Not teasing.

Actually scared.

Luna stared at him silently.

Meanwhile Zayn laughed softly to hide the pain inside his voice.

“Stupid question ah?”

But his eyes betrayed him completely.

Because deep inside—

he already noticed it.

The way Luna’s eyes changed whenever Kyros appeared.

The confusion.

The emotions.

The pain.

Things she never showed anyone else.

Zayn swallowed hard before continuing quietly.

“I know you forgot your past…”

His voice softened.

“But when you look at him…”

He looked down briefly.

“…it feels like some part of you still remembers.”

Luna’s chest tightened painfully after hearing that.

Immediately—

“No—”

But Zayn shook his head softly.

“I’m trying, Luna.”

His voice cracked slightly.

“Really trying not to think like this.”

The tears in Luna’s eyes slowly formed again.

Because she never realized—

Zayn was hurting too.

Zayn smiled weakly.

“But today when you said his name…”

He laughed painfully.

“…I got scared.”

Silence.

Complete silence.

Then slowly—

Luna walked closer toward him.

And without hesitation—

held his face gently.

Zayn looked at her quietly.

Luna’s eyes filled completely now.

“I’m not leaving you.”

Her voice trembled softly.

“I promise.”

Zayn stared at her for a few seconds like he desperately wanted to believe that.

Then quietly—

“What if your memories come back?”

Luna’s breath caught.

Zayn’s eyes looked unbearably vulnerable now.

“What if one day…”

His voice became smaller.

“…you remember loving him more?”

The tears finally escaped Luna’s eyes completely.

Because hearing fear in Zayn’s voice hurt her deeply.

Immediately—

she hugged him tightly.

So tightly.

Like she wanted to erase every insecurity inside him.

Zayn closed his eyes instantly while holding her back.

And against his chest—

Luna whispered emotionally—

“I’m here.”

Her fingers tightened against his shirt.

“And I’m not going anywhere.”

Zayn’s arms wrapped around her even tighter after hearing that.

But somewhere deep inside—

fear still remained quietly inside his heart.
