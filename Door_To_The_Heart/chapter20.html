✦ Chapter Twenty ✦

The Diary.

✦

Morning sunlight filled the apartment softly.

Luna stood near the mirror nervously fixing her formal shirt for the hundredth time.

Meanwhile, Zayn leaned against the door watching her with amusement.

“You’re going to scare your new company if you keep looking this stressed.”

Luna immediately glared at him through the mirror.

“I’m NOT stressed.”

“You changed your hairstyle four times.”

“That’s different.”

“That’s literally stress.”

Luna grabbed a hairbrush and threw it toward him.

Zayn caught it easily while laughing.

“Violence before office hours? Wow.”

Luna sighed dramatically before sitting down on the couch.

“What if they hate me?”

Zayn walked closer immediately.

“No one can hate you.”

“That sounds fake.”

“It’s true.”

He gently fixed the collar of her shirt before speaking again.

“You’ll do amazing.”

Luna looked up at him quietly.

Somehow…

his voice always calmed her down.

Zayn noticed her nervous expression and suddenly held her cheeks dramatically.

“Listen carefully, madam.”

Luna blinked.

“You are smart.”

He kissed her forehead softly.

“You are talented.”

Then lightly tapped her nose.

“And if anyone annoys you…”

His eyes narrowed playfully.

“…call me. I’ll fight them.”

Luna burst out laughing immediately.

“You’re so dramatic.”

“But effective.”

She smiled properly now.

And seeing that alone made Zayn relax.

A few minutes later, he dropped her outside the company building.

Before leaving, he lowered the bike visor slightly and pointed at her seriously.

“No overthinking.”

“Yes sir.”

“No skipping lunch.”

“Yes sir.”

“And no falling asleep during orientation.”

Luna gasped.

“That happened ONE time.”

“Three times.”

“Leave.”

Zayn laughed while she walked toward the building pretending to be offended.

But before entering—

Luna turned back once.

Zayn was still waiting there.

Watching until she safely went inside.

And somehow…

that small habit still made her heart feel warm.

Inside the company, everything looked huge.

Bright lights.

Busy employees.

Elegant glass walls.

Luna nervously adjusted her bag strap while looking around.

A cheerful HR manager walked toward her immediately.

“You must be Luna!”

Luna smiled politely.

“Yes.”

“Come, everyone’s waiting.”

Soon, she was introduced to the entire team.

Everyone seemed friendly.

Some employees welcomed her warmly.

Others joked around casually.

And slowly—

Luna started relaxing.

Maybe today wouldn’t be that bad after all.

“This is your workspace,” the manager explained while showing her around.

Luna nodded carefully, trying to remember everything.

Then suddenly—

the atmosphere around the office changed.

Employees straightened slightly.

Some immediately stood up.

Others lowered their voices.

Luna frowned in confusion.

“What happened?”

Before anyone answered—

she turned.

And froze.

Her heartbeat stopped instantly.

Because walking through the office hallway—

was Kyros.

Black suit.

Cold expression.

Sharp eyes.

The entire office silently watched him with respect as he walked forward calmly.

But the moment his eyes landed on Luna—

he stopped.

Everything inside Luna went numb.

No.

No no no.

Her breathing became uneven immediately.

The HR manager smiled nervously beside her.

“Our CEO.”

CEO.

The word echoed loudly inside her head.

Kyros was the owner of the company.

Luna stared at him in complete shock.

And for one painful second—

Kyros looked shocked too.

Like he never expected to see her there.

The entire office stayed silent awkwardly.

Because something about the way they looked at each other felt too personal.

Too emotional.

But Kyros quickly hid it.

His face turned unreadable again.

Professional.

Cold.

Like the man who once looked at her with unbearable love no longer existed.

“Welcome to the company,” he said calmly.

His voice sounded normal.

Too normal.

And somehow…

that hurt Luna more.

Because this time—

he truly acted like a stranger.

That evening, Luna returned home emotionally exhausted.

The moment she entered the apartment, she quietly dropped her bag onto the sofa before sitting down silently in the hall.

Everything felt heavy today.

Kyros being her CEO.

The way he avoided looking at her longer than necessary.

The way her heart still reacted around him.

None of it made sense anymore.

A little later, the front door opened again.

Zayn walked inside carrying snacks casually.

The moment he saw Luna curled up quietly on the couch, his expression softened.

“Tough first day?”

Luna sighed softly.

“You have no idea.”

Zayn sat beside her while Luna slowly leaned against his shoulder tiredly.

Then his eyes suddenly noticed the old black diary lying on the table.

“Oh?” he picked it up curiously. “You finally took this out?”

Luna immediately looked at it.

The moment she saw the diary—

her expression changed slightly.

Not shock.

Something quieter.

More emotional.

— End of Chapter 20 —
