✦ Chapter Thirteen ✦

Please Stop Looking At Me Like That.

✦

The rain poured harder now.

Cold.

Heavy.

Merciless.

But Luna couldn’t feel any of it anymore.

Because the way Kyros looked at her was hurting far more than the storm ever could.

Like she was someone he loved so much…

and lost so cruelly.

Luna’s breathing became shaky.

“I…” Her voice cracked badly. “I don’t understand any of this…”

Kyros took a small step closer immediately.

“Luna—”

“No.”

She stepped back fast.

Tears mixed with rain across her face.

“No, please… don’t.”

Kyros’ expression slowly fell apart watching her cry.

But Luna couldn’t stop.

Because deep inside her chest, something felt painfully wrong.

Like her heart knew him.

Even if her mind didn’t.

“I swear…” she whispered helplessly, “I swear I don’t remember you…”

Kyros closed his eyes for one second.

As if hearing that physically hurt him.

Then he looked back at her again.

Still soft.

Still patient.

Even after everything.

“I know,” he whispered.

That made her cry harder.

Because why was he being gentle?

Why did he look at her like she mattered more than his own pain?

Luna suddenly grabbed her head tightly.

“I never saw you before…” she cried. “I don’t know who you are…”

Kyros immediately moved toward her.

“Hey, hey— calm down—”

“No!” she shouted through tears. “Stop talking to me like we shared something!”

Her knees finally gave out.

She collapsed onto the wet road, crying so hard she could barely breathe.

People nearby slowly started staring.

But Luna didn’t care.

“I don’t remember anything…” she sobbed painfully. “Nothing…”

Kyros’ face shattered completely.

Seeing her like this clearly destroyed him.

He immediately knelt down in front of her despite the rain soaking him completely.

“Luna, look at me.”

She shook her head violently.

“I can’t… I can’t…”

“You don’t have to remember right now.”

His voice trembled for the first time.

“But please stop crying like this…”

Luna looked up at him with red eyes.

“Then stop looking at me like I broke your heart!”

Silence.

Kyros couldn’t answer.

Because she had.

And somehow that truth was written all over his face.

Luna cried harder after seeing it.

“I’m sorry…” she whispered repeatedly. “I’m sorry… I’m sorry…”

Kyros’ eyes filled instantly.

God.

She was apologizing without even knowing what for.

He carefully reached for her shaking hands.

“You have nothing to apologize for.”

“But you look hurt!” she cried. “Every time you talk to me you look hurt and I don’t even know why!”

Kyros looked away for a moment, jaw tightening painfully.

Like he was trying his hardest not to completely break down in front of her.

Then quietly—

“Because I missed you.”

Those four words destroyed whatever strength Luna had left.

She covered her mouth and cried harder.

Kyros immediately moved closer.

“Hey… hey, please don’t do this to yourself…”

But Luna kept shaking her head.

“I can’t remember you…”

Her voice came out tiny.

Broken.

“And I think that’s killing you.”

Kyros looked at her like his heart had stopped beating.

Rainwater dripped from his hair as he stared at the girl he loved crying right in front of him…

because she couldn’t remember loving him back.

Before he could speak again—

“LUNA!”

A voice suddenly cut through the rain.

Both of them turned.

A young man ran toward them quickly, panic written all over his face.

The moment he saw Luna crying on the ground, his expression darkened instantly.

“Who the hell are you?”

He immediately pulled Luna slightly behind him protectively.

Kyros stood up slowly.

The boy looked furious.

“What did you say to her?”

Kyros stayed silent.

Luna weakly grabbed her friend’s sleeve.

“Please…”

But the boy ignored her completely, glaring at Kyros.

“She’s not mentally okay right now. Don’t mess with her.”

Kyros’ expression changed hearing that.

Pain flashed across his face immediately.

Not pity.

Pain.

Like hearing those words about Luna hurt him deeply.

“I’m not trying to hurt her,” he said quietly.

“Then stay away from her.”

The warning came out cold.

Sharp.

Protective.

Luna felt dizzy suddenly, her vision blurring from crying too much.

The boy quickly held her before she collapsed completely.

“Luna?”

Kyros instinctively stepped forward.

But the boy snapped immediately.

“Don’t touch her.”

The words hit harder than they should have.

Kyros froze.

Luna looked at him weakly through blurred vision.

And somehow…

the pain in his eyes looked lonelier than the rain around him.

Before she lost consciousness completely—

she saw him standing there alone.

Still looking at her like losing her once had already ruined him enough.
