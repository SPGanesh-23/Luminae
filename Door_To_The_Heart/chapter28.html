✦ Chapter Twenty-Eight ✦

“Please Come Back.”

✦

The city felt too big tonight.

Too loud.

Too empty.

Luna walked quickly through crowded streets while continuously calling Zayn again and again.

Still no answer.

Her fingers trembled slightly holding the phone tighter.

“Please…”

Nothing.

Only silence.

The cold night wind brushed against her face softly while fear slowly started growing heavier inside her chest.

Because this didn’t feel normal.

Zayn never disappeared like this.

Never.

Two hours later—

Luna stood outside a small convenience store breathing tiredly.

“Did you see him?”

The shop owner shook his head.

“No ma.”

Another dead end.

Luna whispered a soft thank you before walking away again.

Her legs hurt.

Her head hurt.

Everything hurt.

But she kept searching.

Because what if he was alone somewhere?

What if he was hurting?

What if—

Her thoughts suddenly stopped when she noticed a familiar black hoodie hanging outside a bike nearby.

Zayn’s favorite hoodie color.

Immediately her chest tightened.

Flashback.

“Black suits me more right?”“No. You look like a walking depression quote.”

Luna laughed accidentally remembering it.

But the laugh disappeared quickly too.

Because now—

that memory hurt.

Everything reminded her of him.

The coffee shop where he waited for her after work.

The roadside food stall he liked.

The stupid cat near his apartment that only listened to him.

Every little thing suddenly carried Zayn everywhere.

And slowly—

fear became unbearable.

What if he actually leaves me ?

That thought alone made her breathing shake.

Meanwhile—

inside a parked car nearby—

Kyros silently watched Luna from a distance.

His hands tightened slightly around the steering wheel seeing her search desperately like this.

Tired.

Scared.

Broken.

He closed his eyes briefly.

Because even now—

her tears still destroyed him.

One of his men leaned slightly forward from the front seat.

“Sir, we found nothing yet.”

Kyros immediately opened his eyes again.

“Keep searching.”

“Sir—”

“I said keep searching.”

His voice cracked slightly this time.

Not angry.

Just exhausted.

Because even after everything—

if Luna cared about someone…

Kyros couldn’t ignore it.

Even if that person was Zayn.

A little later—

Luna slowly sat down at an empty bus stop.

Too exhausted to continue walking.

Her eyes looked swollen now from crying silently all night.

She checked her phone again.

No calls.

No messages.

Nothing.

And suddenly—

she broke.

A small sob escaped before she quickly covered her mouth.

“Why does everyone keep leaving me…?”

Her voice sounded tiny.

Fragile.

Like a scared child.

And immediately—

a memory flashed suddenly inside her head.

Bright sunlight.

Laughter.

Kyros driving.

Zayn sitting behind.

Her laughing loudly.

All three together.

FLASH.

Luna’s eyes widened instantly.

Her breathing stopped for one second.

“What…”

Another flash.

Kyros looking at Zayn angrily.

“Take care of her.”

Then blood.

Broken glass.

Loud ringing sound.

FLASH.

Luna grabbed her head immediately.

Pain shot through her mind sharply.

“Ah—”

Tears filled her eyes instantly.

Because none of these memories made sense.

Why were Kyros and Zayn together before?

What happened that night?

Why did everything feel incomplete?

Suddenly—

a shadow stopped in front of her.

Luna slowly looked up.

And froze.

Kyros.

His eyes immediately softened after seeing her cry.

Without saying anything—

he quietly sat beside her.

The silence between them felt heavy.

But strangely comforting too.

Luna wiped her tears quickly.

“I don’t need your pity.”

Kyros looked at her quietly.

“It’s not pity.”

His voice sounded tired too.

Luna stared down at her shaking hands.

Then softly—

“Everyone keeps leaving me.”

The words came out more broken than she intended.

Kyros’s expression immediately changed.

Pain flashed across his face instantly.

Because he knew—

deep down—

he was part of that pain too.

For a few seconds—

he said nothing.

Then finally—

very quietly—

“I came back didn’t I?”

Luna’s breathing shook again immediately.

Because somehow—

that line hurt more than anything else tonight.

Silence filled the bus stop softly.

Cold wind moved around them.

And for the first time in years—

they both looked equally broken.

Suddenly—

Luna’s phone vibrated.

Both of them looked down immediately.

Voice message from Zayn.

Luna’s fingers trembled while opening it.

Then slowly—

Zayn’s tired voice played.

“I just wanted to know something…”

Static sound.

Then quietly—

“If I disappeared…”

A painful pause.

“…would you search for me too?”

The voice message ended.

And Luna’s eyes filled instantly.
