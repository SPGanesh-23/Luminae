✦ Chapter Fourteen ✦

She Forgot You.

✦

The hospital hallway felt unbearably cold.

White lights.

White walls.

White silence.

Everything around Luna looked lifeless.

Machines beeped softly beside her hospital bed while rain tapped endlessly against the window nearby.

Her mother sat beside her with trembling hands wrapped around Luna’s fingers like she was afraid to let go.

“What happened to my daughter…?” she whispered tearfully.

Across the room, Zayn stood completely soaked from the rain, exhausted both physically and emotionally.

He looked toward Luna for a moment before answering quietly.

“She collapsed outside.”

Her mother immediately stood up in panic.

“Collapsed? Why?”

Zayn opened his mouth…

then stopped.

Because how was he supposed to explain any of this?

How could he explain the way Luna cried like her heart was being torn apart in front of a stranger?

“She was talking to someone,” he finally muttered.

“Who?”

Zayn’s jaw tightened.

“I don’t know.”

But that wasn’t true anymore.

Because the image of Kyros refusing to leave Luna in the rain kept replaying inside his head.

The way that man looked at her…

It wasn’t normal.

It looked painful.

Too painful.

Before another word could be spoken—

the hospital room door suddenly opened.

Zayn turned immediately.

And his expression darkened.

Kyros stood there silently.

His clothes were still wet from the storm.

Dark hair dripping water onto the hospital floor.

But none of that mattered.

Because the moment his eyes landed on Luna lying unconscious on the bed—

his entire expression broke.

Like someone had stabbed something directly into his chest.

He took a slow step forward instinctively.

“Luna…”

Her name left his lips so softly it almost sounded like a prayer.

Zayn immediately blocked his path.

“Don’t.”

Kyros looked at him slowly.

For a second, he didn’t even seem angry.

Just desperate.

“I need to see her.”

“She fainted because of you.”

The words hit hard.

But Kyros barely reacted.

His eyes never left Luna.

Pale.

Weak.

Silent.

And somehow that silence seemed to be destroying him more every second.

“I just want to know if she’s okay,” he whispered.

Zayn laughed bitterly.

“You suddenly care now?”

That finally made Kyros look at him.

And God—

his eyes looked ruined.

Like he hadn’t slept properly in years.

Like loving Luna had slowly become the saddest part of his life.

“You think I stopped caring?” he asked quietly.

Zayn went silent for a second.

Because that voice…

That wasn’t guilt alone.

That was grief.

Real grief.

The kind people carry after losing someone they never emotionally recovered from.

But Zayn still stepped in front of the room protectively.

“You’re not going near her.”

Kyros clenched his jaw painfully.

“And who are you to decide that?”

“Someone who stayed.”

Silence.

Heavy.

Sharp.

Kyros looked like those words physically hurt him.

Then quietly—

“Who are you to her?”

Zayn stared at him coldly.

But before answering, he glanced toward Luna sleeping weakly on the hospital bed.

Then back at Kyros.

“At least she remembers me.”

Kyros froze completely.

That single sentence shattered every emotion on his face.

Zayn continued mercilessly.

“You keep asking why she forgot you?”

His voice cracked angrily now.

“She didn’t choose to.”

Kyros stopped breathing.

Zayn stepped closer.

“She has memory loss.”

The world around Kyros seemed to collapse silently.

His eyes widened slowly.

Like his mind rejected the words immediately.

“No…”

But Zayn didn’t stop.

“She lost her memories after the accident.”

Accident.

That word destroyed him.

Completely.

Kyros took a small step back without realizing it.

His face slowly drained of color.

And for the first time—

he looked terrified.

Not angry.

Not cold.

Terrified.

Like his worst fear had just become real.

Zayn’s eyes burned with anger.

“Do you know what she went through after that?”

Kyros couldn’t answer.

Because suddenly every moment from tonight replayed in his head.

The confusion in Luna’s eyes.

The fear in her voice.

The way she cried saying:

“I don’t remember you…”

God.

She wasn’t lying.

She truly didn’t remember him.

Zayn laughed bitterly through his anger.

“She forgot everything.”

Kyros’ breathing became uneven.

“Her memories.”

“Her past.”

“Pieces of herself.”

Each word sounded like another knife twisting deeper into him.

“And you…”

Zayn’s voice lowered dangerously.

“…you were part of what she lost.”

Kyros looked toward Luna again instantly.

His eyes slowly filled.

Not dramatic tears.

The kind that appear when someone’s heart quietly gives up trying to stay strong.

Luna slept peacefully now.

Completely unaware that the person standing outside her room looked like he was falling apart just by looking at her.

Kyros swallowed hard.

“What happened to her…?” he whispered weakly.

Zayn’s expression hardened immediately.

“You don’t deserve to know.”

That sentence broke something inside Kyros completely.

Because maybe Zayn was right.

Maybe he didn’t deserve to stand here after whatever pain Luna suffered because of him.

A shaky breath escaped his lips.

Then suddenly—

he laughed.

Softly.

Brokenly.

Like he couldn’t believe fate could be this cruel.

All this time…

he thought Luna abandoned him.

But she didn’t.

She lost herself too.

Kyros looked at her through the glass window with eyes full of unbearable love and regret.

The girl who once knew every part of him…

now looked at him like a stranger.

And somehow—

that hurt more than losing her.

Before he could move closer, two men in black suits rushed down the hallway.

“Sir.”

Kyros ignored them completely.

“Sir, we need to leave.”

Still nothing.

His eyes remained fixed on Luna.

One of the bodyguards lowered his voice carefully.

“It’s dangerous for you to stay here.”

Kyros finally spoke.

Quietly.

Almost empty.

“I spent years trying to find her.”

His eyes never left Luna.

“And now I’m standing right in front of her…”

His voice cracked painfully.

“…but she can’t even remember loving me.”

Even Zayn went silent hearing that.

The bodyguards exchanged worried looks.

“Sir—”

Kyros shut his eyes tightly.

Like he was trying not to completely break down inside the hospital hallway.

Then Zayn’s cold voice came again.

“Stay away from her.”

Kyros slowly opened his eyes after that.

And for the first time since arriving—

he looked truly defeated.
