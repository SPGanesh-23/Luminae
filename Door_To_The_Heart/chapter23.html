✦ Chapter Twenty-Three ✦

Some Memories Feel Too Warm.

✦

The apartment was silent.

Only the ticking clock and soft page-turning sounds filled the room while Luna sat curled up on the couch holding the diary carefully.

She told herself she would stop after one page.

But somehow—

she kept reading.

Because every memory inside this diary felt alive.

Warm.

Painfully warm.

Slowly—

she turned another page.

And immediately—

a tiny polaroid slipped out.

Luna picked it up carefully.

It was her and Kyros.

Kyros stood behind her while Luna looked annoyed at the camera because he was kissing her cheek unexpectedly.

But despite acting angry—

she was smiling.

Brightly.

Comfortably.

Like she belonged there.

Luna’s chest tightened strangely.

Then underneath the picture—

a sentence was written.

“We fought over the dumbest things.”

And slowly—

the memory pulled her in again.

Five Years Before The Accident…

“You’re cheating.”

Luna glared across the arcade game machine dramatically while Kyros looked completely unbothered.

“I’m winning.”

“That is NOT the same thing.”

“It usually is.”

Luna gasped loudly.

“You distracted me on purpose!”

Kyros looked thoughtful.

“You got distracted because you were already losing.”

“I WAS WINNING.”

“You crashed your car into the wall three times.”

“That wall came suddenly.”

Kyros laughed immediately.

And honestly?

That laugh alone made Luna want to fight him more.

Because WHY did he look this attractive while laughing at her suffering?

“No,” Luna said while pointing at him accusingly. “Play again.”

Kyros raised an eyebrow.

“You already lost twice.”

“Best out of five.”

“That’s not how losing works.”

“Play again.”

Kyros smiled softly.

God.

That smile.

Luna hated how weak it made her heart.

“Fine,” he said finally.

Ten minutes later—

Luna lost again.

Silence.

Complete silence.

Kyros slowly looked toward her.

Luna stared at the game screen like her entire bloodline got betrayed.

Then finally—

“This machine hates me.”

Kyros burst out laughing immediately.

“Oh my God stop laughing.”

“You blamed the machine.”

“Because it’s suspicious!”

Kyros laughed harder while Luna crossed her arms angrily.

“You’re enjoying this too much.”

“You’re cute when you’re angry.”

Luna froze for one second.

Then immediately looked away.

“Don’t flirt after humiliating me.”

“I’m serious.”

“That’s worse.”

Kyros smiled softly before walking closer toward her.

Meanwhile Luna continued pretending to be offended dramatically.

“I don’t want to talk to you anymore.”

“You said that thirty minutes ago too.”

“And I meant it.”

“But you still stayed.”

Luna opened her mouth—

then closed it immediately.

Because annoyingly…

he was right again.

Kyros looked unfairly pleased about that.

“I hate when you look proud.”

“I earned it.”

“You cheated.”

“You’re repeating yourself now.”

Luna glared harder.

Then suddenly—

she started walking away dramatically toward the mall exit.

“I’m leaving.”

Kyros followed calmly beside her.

“You’re still angry?”

“Yes.”

“You lost a game, Luna.”

“I lost with dignity.”

“You screamed at a child earlier.”

“HE PUSHED ME.”

Kyros laughed quietly again.

Then softly—

“Okay, okay. Come here.”

Before Luna could react properly—

Kyros suddenly pulled her gently closer by the waist.

Luna gasped immediately.

“Kyros—”

“You’re pouting.”

“I am NOT.”

“You literally are.”

Luna tried to look away stubbornly while Kyros smiled at her softly.

Then suddenly—

he bent slightly and lifted her into his arms effortlessly.

Luna’s eyes widened instantly.

“KYROS?!”

People nearby turned to look while Luna aggressively held onto his shoulders in panic.

“Put me DOWN.”

“No.”

“Everyone is looking!”

“Let them.”

Luna’s face turned completely red.

“You’re embarrassing me!”

Kyros looked completely calm carrying her through the mall like this was normal behavior.

“You were acting dramatic.”

“So your solution was kidnapping?!”

Kyros laughed softly.

“It’s called pampering.”

“That is NOT pampering!”

“It is to me.”

Luna tried very hard not to smile.

Very hard.

Unfortunately—

Kyros noticed immediately.

“There’s the smile.”

Luna instantly hid her face against his shoulder.

“Shut up.”

Kyros smiled softly while adjusting his hold on her carefully.

And quietly—

“There she is.”

The teasing disappeared from his voice now.

Only affection remained.

Like calming Luna down mattered naturally to him.

After a few seconds, Luna slowly peeked at him again.

Still embarrassed.

Still shy.

And somehow—

Kyros looked at her like she was the cutest thing in the world.

That look alone made her heart weak every single time.

Present Time…

The memory faded slowly.

And suddenly—

Luna closed the diary immediately.

Too fast.

Too emotional.

Her breathing felt uneven now.

Why did these memories hurt this much?

Why did seeing Kyros love her so gently make her chest ache unbearably?

Luna pressed her fingers against her eyes tightly.

“No…”

Her voice cracked softly.

“I can’t do this.”

Because every page made her miss a love she couldn’t even remember.

And somehow—

that pain felt worse than forgetting.

Luna looked at the diary one last time.

Then stood up suddenly.

Angrily.

Emotionally.

Before she could change her mind—

she walked toward the kitchen and threw the diary inside the dustbin.

The sound echoed loudly inside the apartment.

Silence followed immediately after.

Luna stood there breathing shakily.

Trying to convince herself she did the right thing.

Trying to ignore the painful feeling growing inside her chest.

But even after throwing the diary away—

her heart still remembered the warmth of his arms.
