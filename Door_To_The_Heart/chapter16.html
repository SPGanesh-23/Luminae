✦ Chapter Sixteen ✦

You Loved Him.

✦

Darkness surrounded Luna softly.

Not painful.

Not scary.

Just… quiet.

And somewhere inside that silence—

she heard laughter.

Familiar laughter.

Warm fingers slowly intertwining with hers.

A voice beside her.

“You overthink too much.”

Luna turned slightly.

And there he was.

Kyros.

Smiling softly at her under golden evening lights.

Not broken.

Not hurting.

Just happy.

The sight alone made her chest ache strangely.

“You’re staring again,” he teased quietly.

Luna rolled her eyes.

“You talk too much.”

Kyros gasped dramatically.

“That’s rude. I’m literally your boyfriend.”

Luna laughed.

A real laugh.

Light.

Carefree.

And the moment she laughed, Kyros looked at her like she was the most beautiful thing he had ever seen.

God.

The way he looked at her…

Like loving her was the easiest thing in the world.

He suddenly leaned closer.

“What?”

Luna blinked nervously.

Kyros smiled teasingly.

“You get shy so easily.”

“I do not.”

“You do.”

“I hate you.”

Kyros laughed softly.

“No you don’t.”

Then quieter—

“You love me too much for that.”

Her heart fluttered painfully.

Because somehow…

that felt true.

Kyros gently tucked her hair behind her ear.

“You know what your problem is, Luna?”

She frowned suspiciously.

“What?”

“You always act strong when you’re actually soft.”

Luna opened her mouth to argue—

—but suddenly his expression changed.

Softened.

Like he wanted to remember this exact version of her forever.

“I miss this smile,” he whispered.

Luna’s heartbeat stopped.

"Miss?"

Before she could ask—

everything shattered.

The laughter disappeared.

The warmth disappeared.

The light disappeared.

And suddenly—

Luna’s eyes snapped open.

A sharp breath escaped her lips as she sat up quickly on the hospital bed.

Her chest rose and fell rapidly.

That dream—

No.

It didn’t feel like a dream.

It felt real.

Too real.

Her mother immediately rushed toward her.

“Luna!”

Luna looked around anxiously.

The hospital room.

The machines.

The rain outside.

Reality.

But her heart still hurt.

Like she had lost something important again.

Her mother gently touched her face.

“Are you okay?”

Luna looked at her slowly.

Then quietly—

“Where’s Zayn?”

Her mother looked slightly confused by the sudden question but nodded anyway.

“I’ll call him.”

A few minutes later, Zayn entered the room carefully.

The moment he saw Luna awake, relief immediately crossed his face.

“You scared everyone.”

But Luna didn’t smile.

She just stared at him.

Waiting.

Zayn instantly noticed the look in her eyes.

And he already knew where this conversation was going.

“No,” he said immediately.

Luna frowned.

“What?”

“You need rest.”

“Zayn.”

“Luna—”

“Please.”

That single word stopped him.

Because her voice sounded fragile.

Confused.

Almost desperate.

Luna swallowed hard before speaking again.

“That guy…”

Even saying it made her chest tighten.

“Who is he?”

Zayn looked away immediately.

Avoiding.

Hesitating.

Which only scared Luna more.

“Why does he talk to me like he knows me?”

Still silence.

“Why did seeing him cry hurt me so much?”

Zayn clenched his jaw tightly.

“Luna…”

“Tell me the truth.”

Her voice cracked.

“Please.”

The room became painfully quiet.

Then finally—

Zayn sat down beside her slowly.

His eyes softened sadly.

“You really don’t remember anything…”

Luna felt fear crawl into her chest.

“What do I not remember?”

Zayn looked down for a second before answering.

“You and Kyros…”

His voice became quieter.

“…were together.”

Luna froze completely.

The world around her stopped moving.

“What?”

“You dated.”

No.

No no no.

That couldn’t be true.

Because if it was—

why did her heart react like it already knew?

Zayn continued carefully.

“You loved him a lot.”

Luna’s breathing became uneven.

Fragments of feelings crashed inside her chest painfully.

Warm hands.

Laughter.

His smile.

The way he looked at her.

Tears slowly filled her eyes again.

Zayn’s voice lowered.

“There was an accident.”

That word immediately made her stomach twist.

“After that…”

He swallowed hard.

“…you lost your memories.”

Luna stared at him silently.

Like her brain refused to process the words.

Then suddenly—

everything from the dream replayed inside her head again.

“I’m literally your boyfriend.”

Her hands started shaking instantly.

“Oh my God…”

Zayn immediately moved closer.

“Hey—”

“I really forgot him…?”

Her voice broke completely.

Zayn’s expression turned heartbroken after hearing that.

Because Luna looked devastated by her own memories now.

Or lack of them.

Tears rolled down her cheeks rapidly.

“He looked at me like I destroyed him…”

Zayn immediately pulled her into a hug before she could break down further.

“It’s not your fault.”

Luna cried against his shoulder quietly.

“But he waited for me…”

That sentence shattered something in Zayn’s expression too.

Because even he could see it now.

No matter how angry he was at Kyros—

that man truly loved Luna.
