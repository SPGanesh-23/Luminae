✦ Chapter Twenty-One ✦

Our First Meeting Was Terrible.

✦

The apartment was quiet.

Too quiet.

Soft yellow light illuminated the old diary resting on Luna’s lap while midnight silence wrapped around the room gently.

Across the hall, Zayn had already fallen asleep on the couch after staying beside her for hours.

But Luna couldn’t sleep.

Not after today.

Not after seeing Kyros again.

Her fingers slowly brushed against the diary cover before carefully opening the first page.

Messy handwriting greeted her immediately.

Her handwriting.

“Page 1 — Our first meeting was terrible.”

Luna blinked slowly.

Then underneath it—

another line.

“I genuinely thought he was the most annoying man alive.”

A tiny smile almost appeared on her lips.

Almost.

Then slowly—

she turned the page.

Five Years Before The Accident…

The city was unbearably hot that afternoon.

College students crowded the streets while loud music blasted from nearby shops.

Meanwhile—

Luna stood inside a convenience store arguing with the cashier.

“I gave you five hundred!” Luna complained dramatically.

The cashier looked exhausted already.

“You gave hundred, ma’am.”

Luna blinked.

“No, I definitely gave—”

She suddenly checked her wallet.

Silence.

“…Oh.”

The cashier stared blankly.

Luna awkwardly smiled.

“My bad.”

The cashier sighed deeply like life had already defeated him.

Luna quickly grabbed her iced coffee and rushed outside before embarrassing herself further.

But the moment she stepped out—

someone crashed directly into her shoulder.

The coffee flew instantly.

Straight onto a black shirt.

Everything froze.

Luna stared in horror.

“Oh my God.”

Slowly—

the stranger looked down at the coffee stain spreading across his expensive shirt.

Then finally looked at her.

Sharp eyes.

Cold expression.

Perfect hair.

Definitely rich.

Definitely annoyed.

And unfortunately…

very attractive.

Luna wanted the earth to open and swallow her immediately.

“I can explain,” she said quickly.

The stranger looked at her calmly.

“You attacked me with iced coffee.”

Luna gasped.

“It was an accident!”

“That’s exactly what criminals say.”

“CRIMINAL ah?!”

For the first time, the corner of his lips lifted slightly.

Meanwhile Luna looked personally offended.

“You literally walked into ME!”

The stranger removed his watch calmly before wiping coffee from it.

“You were walking fast enough to start a fire.”

“Because I was escaping embarrassment!”

That made him look mildly amused.

And somehow that irritated Luna even more.

“Why are you smiling?” she asked suspiciously.

“You’re loud.”

“You’re rude.”

“You ruined my shirt.”

“You look rich enough to buy another one.”

The stranger stared at her for one second.

Then suddenly laughed quietly.

A real laugh.

Soft.

Unexpected.

And annoyingly attractive.

Luna blinked once.

Then immediately looked away because absolutely not.

“You’re enjoying this,” she muttered.

“A little,” he admitted calmly.

“You’re insane.”

“And you’re dramatic.”

Before Luna could reply—

a little kid suddenly ran between them accidentally pushing Luna forward.

Everything happened too fast.

Luna lost balance instantly—

and grabbed the stranger’s shirt tightly to stop herself from falling.

Both of them froze.

Too close.

Way too close.

Luna slowly looked up.

And for one second—

everything around them became strangely quiet.

The traffic sounds faded.

The heat disappeared.

Even breathing felt awkward suddenly.

The stranger looked at her silently.

Like he forgot what he was about to say.

Meanwhile Luna’s heartbeat suddenly started acting weird.

Which annoyed her immediately.

After a few seconds, he finally spoke softly.

“…You okay?”

Luna stepped away so fast it almost looked aggressive.

“Yes.”

Too quick.

Too awkward.

The stranger noticed.

And smiled slightly again.

That tiny smile made Luna irrationally angry because WHY was he this attractive while being this annoying?

“I still don’t like you,” Luna muttered.

The stranger raised an eyebrow calmly.

“That’s unfortunate.”

“Why?”

“Because now I’m interested.”

Luna blinked.

“What?”

Before she could process that properly—

a black luxury car stopped beside them.

A driver stepped out immediately.

“Sir, the meeting—”

Luna froze slightly.

Sir?

Oh no.

Rich rich.

The stranger ignored the driver completely and looked back at Luna instead.

“What’s your name?”

Luna crossed her arms immediately.

“Why should I tell you?”

“So I know who ruined my shirt.”

“That shirt deserved it.”

That made him laugh softly again.

God.

That laugh.

Luna hated that laugh already.

He opened the car door calmly before looking at her one last time.

“Then I’ll figure it out myself.”

Luna frowned instantly.

“That sounded creepy.”

“It sounded cooler in my head.”

That unexpected honesty almost made Luna laugh.

Almost.

Before entering the car, he looked at her again.

And strangely—

his expression softened slightly.

Like meeting her accidentally became the best part of his day.

Then quietly—

“My name’s Kyros.”

The car door closed.

And seconds later—

he disappeared into traffic completely.

Luna stood there staring after the car for a few seconds.

Then immediately shook her head.

“Nope.”

Too arrogant.

Too rich.

Too annoying.

She was NEVER seeing that man again.

Unfortunately for her—

fate already decided otherwise.

— End of Chapter 21 —
