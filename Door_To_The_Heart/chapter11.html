The Eyes Filled With Pain

✦

His eyes filled with something deeper than anger.

Pain.

“Why did you break up with me?”

My mind went blank.

Break up…?

Me?

With him?

“No…” I whispered, taking a step back. “You’re wrong… I’ve never even seen you before…”

But even as I said it, my voice didn’t sound convincing.

Not even to me.

He laughed.

Not happily.

Not softly.

It was broken.

“You really don’t remember?” he asked.

His eyes didn’t leave mine.

“Or are you just pretending… again?”

“I’m not pretending!” I snapped, my heart racing. “I don’t know you!”

Silence.

Heavy. Suffocating.

Then slowly… he reached into his pocket.

My breath hitched.

He pulled out his phone.

Opened something.

And turned the screen towards me.

My world shattered.

It was a photo.

Not just any photo.

Me.

Standing next to him.

My head resting on his shoulder.

Smiling like… I belonged there.

“No…” I shook my head, stepping back again. “This is fake… this has to be fake…”

But he didn’t move.

Didn’t argue.

Didn’t shout.

He just said one thing.

Quietly.

“You took that picture.”

My chest tightened.

“On the day you promised,” he continued, his voice barely holding together,

“That no matter what happens…”

My heartbeat roared in my ears.

“…you would never leave me.”

Something inside my head cracked.

A flash.

A door.

His hand holding mine.

My voice—

“I won’t leave you.”

I gasped.

The world spun.

I looked at him again.

Really looked this time.

And suddenly…

His eyes felt familiar.

Too familiar.

“Who… are you…?” I whispered.

But this time—

It wasn’t denial.

It was fear.

Because somewhere deep inside me…

I already knew the answer.
