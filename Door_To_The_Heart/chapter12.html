✦ Chapter Twelve ✦

You Really Don't Remember Me.

✦

The world around them moved normally.

Cars passed by.

People walked through the rain.

Someone laughed in the distance.

But for Luna, everything had gone silent the moment she saw him.

Kyros stood in front of her like a ghost from a life she couldn’t remember living.

And the way he looked at her…

God.

It didn’t look like he had just found someone.

It looked like he had finally found something he had spent years grieving.

Luna slowly stepped back.

Her breathing became uneven.

“No…” she whispered. “This can’t be happening.”

Kyros smiled faintly.

Not a happy smile.

The kind people make when they’re trying not to fall apart.

“You always say that first.”

Luna frowned immediately.

“What?”

His eyes searched hers carefully.

Like he was desperately hoping to find even one piece of the girl he lost.

But whatever he was looking for…

she couldn’t give it to him.

Because she didn’t know him.

At least that’s what she kept telling herself.

“I think you have the wrong person,” she said softly.

The moment those words left her mouth, Kyros looked down for a second.

And Luna swore she saw his shoulders weaken.

Like he was physically tired of being hurt by her.

“You used to get angry when I said things like that,” he whispered.

Luna’s chest tightened painfully.

Why did he sound so real?

Why did every word feel like it belonged somewhere inside her heart already?

Kyros looked back at her slowly.

“Do you really not remember me at all?”

The question wasn’t demanding.

It wasn’t accusing.

That was the worst part.

He sounded terrified of the answer.

Luna opened her mouth—

—but nothing came out.

Because suddenly she noticed something.

His eyes.

They were red.

Not from the rain.

From crying.

Like this wasn’t the first night he had searched for her.

Kyros laughed quietly to himself.

“You know…” he said, voice shaking slightly, “I imagined this moment so many times.”

Luna stood frozen.

“I kept thinking maybe if I saw you again…” He swallowed hard. “Maybe if you looked at me one more time…”

His voice became softer.

“…I’d finally stop missing you.”

A tear rolled down Luna’s cheek before she even realized she was crying.

Kyros noticed immediately.

Of course he did.

That somehow hurt more.

“You still cry the same way,” he whispered.

Luna quickly wiped her tears away.

“Please stop talking like you know me.”

Kyros stared at her for a long moment.

Then finally—

“I knew every version of you.”

That silence after those words felt unbearable.

Rain hit the ground loudly around them.

Luna’s heart wouldn’t stop shaking.

Kyros took a small step closer, careful this time, like he was approaching something fragile.

“You hated thunderstorms,” he said quietly.

Luna looked up slowly.

“You pretended you didn’t.”

A small broken smile appeared on his face.

“But every time lightning struck, you’d move closer to me without noticing.”

Luna felt her breath catch.

Why…

Why did that feel familiar?

Kyros continued looking at her like remembering was painful and beautiful at the same time.

“You used to hold my sleeve whenever you got anxious.”

His eyes slowly lowered toward her trembling hands.

“And when you were trying not to cry…”

His voice almost broke.

“…you’d press your nails into your palm exactly like you’re doing right now.”

Luna immediately loosened her fist.

Fear crawled up her spine.

“How do you know that?”

The question came out smaller than she intended.

Kyros looked at her silently.

And for the first time, Luna saw it clearly.

He wasn’t lying.

Whatever existed between them…

he had truly lived through it.

Kyros smiled weakly.

“You really forgot me.”

Not angry.

Not bitter.

Just… heartbroken.

Like he had been trying to survive that truth for a very long time.

Luna’s tears fell harder now, even though she still didn’t understand why she was crying.

“I’m sorry,” she whispered instinctively.

Kyros’ expression shattered completely after hearing that.

Because that was probably the first thing she had said to him that felt familiar.

He looked away for a second, trying to breathe normally again.

Then he laughed softly through the pain.

“You know what’s cruel?”

Luna stayed silent.

Kyros looked back at her with eyes full of love that clearly never left.

“I spent so long trying to forget you…”

His voice cracked.

“…and you forgot me effortlessly.”
