The Door

✦

The night was unusually silent. Luna lay on her bed, staring into the darkness. Her mind replayed that face again… and again.

Slowly, sleep pulled her in.

A sudden sound. A low… echoing vibration.

Thummm…

Her eyes snapped open.

"What…?"

The room was different. Not dark anymore. There was a soft golden light glowing near her wardrobe.

And then she saw it.

A door.

A door that was never there before.

Her heart started racing.

"This… this isn't real…"

But her feet moved anyway. Step by step. Closer.

The light grew brighter. Her hand trembled as she touched the handle.

Warm.

She swallowed hard… and opened it.

Wind. Fresh air rushed toward her. Her eyes widened.

A vast green field stretched endlessly. Soft grass under her feet. The sky painted in warm sunset colors.

It was… beautiful. Peaceful. Alive.

She stepped forward slowly.

"This place…"

"Beautiful, right?"

A voice. Deep. Gentle. Familiar.

Her breath hitched. She turned.

And there he was.

The boy from the banner. Standing right in front of her.

Real.
