✦ Chapter Eighteen ✦

Five Years Later.

✦

The cold evening wind still lingered around them after the black car disappeared into traffic.

Luna stood frozen in the middle of the sidewalk, staring at the empty road like some part of her was still waiting for him to come back.

But he didn’t.

He left.

Again.

And somehow…

that hurt more than she expected.

Beside her, Zayn watched quietly.

And for the first time since all of this started—

he regretted bringing her outside today.

If he hadn’t insisted…

she wouldn’t have seen Kyros.

She wouldn’t have cried again.

She wouldn’t be standing here looking this broken.

Zayn lowered his eyes slightly before speaking softly.

“I’m sorry.”

Luna slowly looked at him.

“For what?”

Zayn forced a small smile.

“I shouldn’t have brought you here.”

Luna frowned immediately.

“No…”

Her voice became quieter.

“It’s not your fault.”

But even while saying that, her eyes slowly drifted back toward the road again.

Toward the place where Kyros disappeared.

And that hurt Zayn more than he expected.

“Why did he leave like that…?”

The question came out so softly.

So genuinely hurt.

Luna looked down at her hands.

“He looked at me like…”

She struggled to continue.

“Like he wanted to stay.”

Zayn stayed silent.

Because honestly?

He saw it too.

The way Kyros looked at Luna didn’t feel temporary.

It looked permanent.

The kind of love that survives even after being destroyed.

Luna slowly sat back down on the bench, her melted ice cream forgotten completely.

“He didn’t even look back…”

Her voice cracked slightly.

“Did seeing me hurt him that much?”

Zayn’s chest tightened painfully hearing that.

Because no matter how hard he tried—

Luna’s heart still reacted to Kyros naturally.

Like some part of her still belonged there.

The evening sky slowly darkened above them.

And somewhere far away—

a black car disappeared completely into the city lights.

Five Years Later…

Warm sunlight filled the apartment softly.

The sound of laughter echoed through the kitchen while coffee brewed nearby.

Luna stood in front of the mirror fixing her earrings carefully while pretending to look extremely serious.

Meanwhile, Zayn sat nearby watching her with amusement.

Five years.

Life somehow moved forward.

Slowly.

Quietly.

And somewhere during those years, pain turned into comfort.

Comfort turned into attachment.

And attachment became love.

Not dramatic.

Not sudden.

Just soft.

Safe.

Real.

“Madam,” Zayn suddenly called out dramatically.

Luna looked over immediately.

“What?”

“Are you planning to reach office tomorrow or next week?”

Luna gasped.

“Excuse me? I’m getting ready.”

“You’ve been getting ready for the last one hour.”

“That’s called being pretty.”

Zayn laughed softly.

“Ah yes. Very difficult job.”

Luna grabbed the nearest cushion and threw it at him immediately.

Zayn caught it easily while grinning.

“Violence? Before your first day itself?”

Luna crossed her arms proudly.

“You deserve it.”

God.

Seeing her like this still made Zayn feel relieved sometimes.

Because there was a time he genuinely thought Luna would never smile properly again.

Now she laughed easily.

Teased him constantly.

And looked brighter than before.

Zayn stood up slowly before walking toward her.

“Hello madam.”

Luna looked suspicious already.

“What now?”

He fixed his imaginary coat dramatically.

“So tomorrow you are officially joining your new company.”

Luna immediately smiled excitedly.

Zayn bowed slightly like a gentleman.

“All the best, ma’am.”

Luna laughed.

“Thank you, sir.”

“Please remember us after becoming successful.”

Luna pretended to think seriously.

“Hmmm…”

Zayn narrowed his eyes.

She smiled innocently.

“No promises.”

“Wow.”

He placed a hand over his heart dramatically.

“So heartless.”

Luna laughed harder.

The soft sound filled the apartment warmly.

And without realizing it—

Zayn smiled at her the same way people smile when someone slowly becomes their entire world.
