✦ Chapter Twenty-Two ✦

He Started Appearing Everywhere.

✦

The diary rested quietly on Luna’s lap while the apartment stayed silent around her.

She slowly turned the next page.

And immediately—

another sentence greeted her.

“After that day, he started appearing everywhere.”

Underneath it—

“I still don’t know if it was fate… or if Kyros was simply insane.”

A tiny laugh escaped Luna before she could stop it.

Then slowly—

the next memory pulled her in again.

Five Years Before The Accident…

Luna stood inside a bookstore searching for her assignment reference book with pure frustration.

“Where did they even keep this thing…”

She walked through another aisle while mumbling to herself dramatically.

Bad day.

No sleep.

Assignment submission tomorrow.

And now even the book disappeared.

Perfect.

Absolutely perfect.

Luna finally spotted the book on the top shelf.

Her eyes widened immediately.

“Yes!”

She stretched upward trying to grab it.

Almost.

Almost—

Another hand grabbed the book first.

Luna froze.

Slowly turned.

And immediately regretted existing.

Because standing beside her casually holding the book—

was Kyros.

Black shirt.

Relaxed expression.

And that irritating little smile again.

“No,” Luna whispered dramatically.

Kyros looked thoughtful.

“Interesting reaction.”

“What are YOU doing here?”

“I read books sometimes.”

“You look like you buy them for decoration.”

Kyros actually looked offended for one second.

“That hurt.”

“Good.”

He calmly looked at the book in his hands.

“So this is the reason you almost climbed the shelf?”

“I was NOT climbing.”

“You were one step away from risking your life.”

Luna grabbed the book from his hand immediately.

“You’re following me.”

Kyros started walking beside her casually.

“Or maybe you keep appearing wherever I go.”

“That’s impossible because I go to normal places.”

Kyros laughed softly.

God.

That laugh again.

Luna hated how easily it distracted her now.

Both of them continued walking through the bookstore aisles while arguing nonstop.

“You know,” Kyros suddenly said, “you’re less scary today.”

Luna stopped walking immediately.

“SCARY?”

“You yelled at me twice during our first meeting.”

“You deserved worse.”

“Probably.”

Luna narrowed her eyes suspiciously.

“Why do you always agree so calmly?”

Kyros looked at her for a second before answering quietly.

“Because listening to you is interesting.”

The answer came too naturally.

Too honestly.

Luna immediately looked away.

Why did this man speak like that so casually?!

Kyros noticed her avoiding eye contact and smiled slightly to himself.

Cute.

Very cute.

A few minutes later, Luna sat near the reading corner flipping through the book carefully.

Meanwhile Kyros sat directly opposite her like he belonged there.

Luna looked up immediately.

“Why are you sitting here?”

Kyros opened a random book calmly.

“I’m reading.”

“You haven’t turned the page for five minutes.”

“I’m a slow reader.”

“You’re staring at me.”

“That too.”

Luna stared at him in disbelief.

“You admit things too confidently.”

Kyros leaned slightly forward.

“And you get nervous too easily.”

“I’m NOT nervous.”

“Your ears turn red.”

Luna immediately covered her ears.

Kyros laughed quietly again.

And somehow—

that sound made the entire place feel warmer.

After a while, Luna finally packed her things dramatically.

“I’m leaving.”

Kyros stood up immediately.

“Okay.”

Luna looked suspicious already.

“Why are YOU standing?”

“I’m leaving too.”

“You’re literally copying me now.”

“Maybe I enjoy annoying you.”

“You definitely enjoy annoying me.”

“A little.”

They walked outside the bookstore together while sunset light spread softly across the streets.

For once—

the silence between them didn’t feel awkward.

It felt comfortable.

Which honestly scared Luna more.

Kyros suddenly looked at her while walking.

“Can I ask something?”

“What?”

“Why do you always look like you’re preparing to fight me?”

Luna gasped dramatically.

“Because you’re irritating!”

Kyros smiled softly.

“But you still keep talking to me.”

Luna opened her mouth instantly—

then stopped.

Because annoyingly…

he was right.

Kyros looked weirdly pleased seeing her speechless for once.

“I win.”

“You’re impossible.”

“And yet…”

He looked at her quietly for a second too long.

“…you still don’t walk away.”

Luna’s heartbeat stumbled slightly again.

God.

This man was becoming dangerous.

That night—

while writing inside her diary—

Luna stared at the page quietly before smiling to herself without realizing.

Then finally wrote:

“I think meeting Kyros accidentally is slowly becoming my favorite part of the day.”
