✦ Chapter Fifteen ✦

Don't Say Her Name Again.

✦

The inside of the car was silent.

Too silent.

Rainwater slid slowly down the dark windows while the city lights blurred outside like fading memories.

Kyros sat motionless in the backseat, his wet clothes still clinging to him.

But he didn’t notice any of it.

His mind was still inside that hospital hallway.

Still stuck on Luna’s voice.

“I don’t remember you…”

His jaw tightened painfully.

Every time those words replayed in his head, it felt harder to breathe.

The bodyguards sitting in front exchanged nervous glances through the mirror but stayed silent.

No one wanted to speak to him right now.

Because the look in his eyes was enough.

Broken.

Completely broken.

Suddenly—

his phone started ringing.

Kyros slowly looked down at the screen.

Dad.

The moment he answered, an angry voice exploded through the call.

“What the hell were you thinking?!”

Kyros shut his eyes tiredly.

His father continued furiously.

“Do you even realize what kind of scandal this could become if reporters find out?!”

Kyros stayed silent.

“Running around hospitals in the middle of the night for some girl— have you lost your mind?!”

That sentence finally made something inside Kyros snap.

“Don’t call her ‘some girl.’”

The car immediately fell even quieter.

Even the bodyguards looked tense now.

Because Kyros almost never raised his voice at his father.

But the older man only got angrier.

“So it’s true.”

Kyros laughed bitterly.

A broken sound.

“You already knew.”

Silence.

Then quietly—

“What happened to Luna?”

His father didn’t answer.

Kyros gripped the phone harder.

“You know something.”

Still silence.

His breathing became uneven again.

“Then stop hiding it from me and tell me what happened to her!”

The pain in his voice finally came out.

Raw.

Uncontrolled.

“Why does she have memory loss?”

The car suddenly felt suffocating.

His father’s voice became colder after that.

“You should stop asking questions that will only destroy you.”

Kyros’ eyes slowly filled with anger and hurt.

“She cried in front of me today without even knowing why.”

His voice cracked.

“She looked at me like I was a stranger.”

For the first time since the call started, his father became quiet.

And somehow…

that silence scared Kyros more.

“Dad…”

His voice became weaker now.

More desperate.

“Please.”

That single word sounded almost painful.

“What happened that day?”

A long silence followed.

Then finally—

“You still haven’t understood anything, have you?”

Kyros frowned.

His father’s next words came sharp enough to cut.

“The accident happened because you couldn’t stay away from her.”

Kyros froze completely.

“Dad—”

“You ruined that girl’s life.”

The words hit like a slap.

Kyros immediately stopped breathing.

His father continued mercilessly.

“She lost her memories.”

“She lost herself.”

“And even after all that…”

His voice hardened further.

“…you still came back to destroy whatever peace she has left?”

Kyros’ eyes shut tightly.

Like every word physically hurt him.

“No…” he whispered shakily. “I would never hurt her…”

“But you already did.”

Silence.

Heavy.

Deadly silence.

Kyros slowly lowered his head.

Rain tapped softly against the windows while his father’s breathing echoed through the phone.

Then quietly—

“She used to smile differently before meeting you.”

That sentence shattered him.

Completely.

Because deep down…

Kyros loved Luna enough to believe that maybe her pain really was his fault.

His father sighed tiredly from the other side.

“You need to let her go.”

Kyros laughed softly after hearing that.

Not because it was funny.

Because it hurt too much.

“How am I supposed to do that?”

His voice finally broke completely.

“She forgot me…”

His breathing became shaky.

“But I remember everything.”

Silence filled the call again.

Then his father spoke one last time.

“If you truly love her…”

His tone became cold.

“…stay away from her.”

The call disconnected.

Kyros slowly lowered the phone from his ear.

Nobody inside the car spoke.

Nobody moved.

Because the tears in his eyes said enough already.

Outside, the rain kept falling endlessly.

And for the first time in years—

Kyros looked completely alone.
