✦ Chapter Ten ✦

Maybe None of It Was Real

✦

I told myself I wouldn’t go back.

No more doors.

No more him.

It had to stop.

Because none of it was real… right?

The banner was still there.

Same street. Same pole. Same smile.

His eyes followed me again — or maybe my mind was playing tricks.

I looked away quickly.

“Stop it,” I whispered to myself. “He’s not real.”

But my heart didn’t listen.

That night, I didn’t open the door.

I saw it.

Right there.

At the corner of my room.

The same door that wasn’t supposed to exist.

Dark wood. Silver handle.

Waiting.

Always waiting.

My fingers trembled.

Just one step… and I could see him again.

Just one step… and everything would feel real again.

“No.”

I stepped back.

“I’m done.”

Days passed.

Then weeks.

I avoided that street.

I avoided that banner.

I avoided everything that reminded me of him.

But something felt… wrong.

It started small.

A photo.

On my phone.

Me… smiling.

Standing next to him.

My breath hitched.

“No… this is edited… this has to be fake…”

I don’t remember this.

I don’t remember him.

So how—

Then my diary.

I don’t even remember writing in it.

But there it was.

Page after page.

About him.

About us.

About… love.

"He held my hand today."

"He knows me better than anyone."

"I think I’m falling for him."

My hands started shaking.

“This isn’t real… I would remember… right?”

Right?

That night, I couldn’t sleep.

Every time I closed my eyes, I saw flashes.

His smile.

His voice.

His eyes.

And something else.

Pain.

I sat up, breathing heavily.

“I’m losing my mind…”

Maybe that door wasn’t imagination.

Maybe…

No.

I shook my head hard.

“No. This is all fake.”

The next day, I made a decision.

I would prove it.

I walked down that same street again.

Slow steps.

Heavy heart.

And there it was.

The banner.

Him.

Still smiling like he knew something I didn’t.

I looked up.

“Who are you…?” I whispered.

“Me?”

The voice came from behind me.

Real.

Close.

Too close.

My body froze.

Slowly… I turned.

And my world stopped.

It was him.

Not the banner.

Not a dream.

Not behind a door.

Right in front of me.

Real.

Breathing.

Looking at me like—

Like I broke him.

My lips parted.

But no words came out.

My heart pounded so loud it hurt.

He took a step closer.

His eyes searching mine.

Hurt. Confused. Angry.

“Why are you looking at me like that…?” he asked softly.

I couldn’t breathe.

“I… I don’t even know you…”

Something in his expression shattered.

“Don’t do that,” he said.

His voice cracked.

“Don’t pretend.”

“I’m not—”

“Then why did you leave me?”

My mind went blank.

“What…?”
