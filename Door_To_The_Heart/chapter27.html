✦ Chapter Twenty-Seven ✦

“You Moved On Without Me?”

✦

Kyros held Luna tightly like letting go would destroy him completely.

And quietly—

almost broken—

“I missed you so much.”

For one dangerous second—

Luna’s eyes slowly closed.

Because his embrace felt painfully familiar.

Warm.

Safe.

Like her heart remembered him even if her mind didn’t.

But suddenly—

reality hit her again.

Luna quickly pushed Kyros away.

Kyros froze immediately.

Luna stepped back breathing unevenly while tears slowly filled her eyes.

“Why are you doing this…?”

Her voice trembled emotionally.

“You suddenly came back and started shaking everything again…”

Kyros stared at her helplessly.

Meanwhile Luna’s emotions finally started spilling out.

“Do you even know how confused I feel?!”

Her breathing became unstable.

“You look at me like I’m important to you…”

A tear rolled down.

“But where were you all these years?”

Silence.

That question hit Kyros harder than anything else.

Because honestly?

He asked himself the same thing every single day.

Kyros looked down briefly before speaking softly—

“For you.”

Luna froze.

Kyros laughed weakly.

“Everything I did…”

His voice cracked slightly.

“…was for you.”

Luna’s chest tightened painfully.

Because the pain in his voice sounded too real.

Too heavy.

Kyros slowly stepped closer again.

This time carefully.

Like he was scared she’d disappear.

“I know you don’t remember anything…”

His eyes slowly filled now too.

“But I never stopped loving you.”

Luna immediately looked away because hearing that hurt too much.

Kyros swallowed hard before whispering—

“And I’m not leaving again.”

Then slowly—

he moved closer like he wanted to hold her again.

But immediately—

“No.”

Luna stepped back quickly.

Kyros froze.

Luna wiped her tears shakily.

“That can’t happen.”

Her voice became smaller now.

“…I’m already in a relationship.”

Silence.

Kyros’s expression slowly changed.

Pain.

Confusion.

Then quietly—

“…Who?”

Luna looked down briefly before answering.

“Zayn.”

Everything inside Kyros stopped.

“…Zayn?”

His voice sounded empty now.

Luna nodded slowly.

Kyros stared at her silently for a few seconds like his brain refused to process it.

Then suddenly—

he laughed softly.

Not happily.

Broken.

“Zayn…”

His jaw tightened slightly.

“Your friend Zayn?”

Luna frowned immediately.

“Don’t say it like that.”

Kyros looked away briefly before speaking again.

“So after everything…”

His voice slowly became colder.

“…you chose him?”

“He stayed with me!”

Luna shouted emotionally.

“When nobody else did!”

Kyros’s eyes darkened painfully.

“And he still accepted you after knowing everything?”

Luna froze slightly.

Kyros laughed bitterly now.

“Interesting.”

Luna immediately sensed the insult hidden inside his tone.

“What does that mean?”

Kyros looked at her directly.

“You forgot me.”

His voice became sharper emotionally.

“But he knew.”

Luna’s chest tightened.

“And still…”

Kyros smiled painfully.

“…he got close to you.”

“Stop it.”

Luna’s voice trembled now.

But Kyros couldn’t stop anymore.

Maybe pain was finally turning into anger.

“Did he comfort you while knowing you belonged to someone else?”

SLAP.

The sound echoed loudly between them.

Kyros froze completely.

Luna’s hand trembled violently after hitting him.

Tears streamed down her face now.

“Don’t you dare talk about him like that.”

Silence.

Complete silence.

Kyros slowly turned back toward her.

Shock still visible in his eyes.

Meanwhile Luna cried angrily.

“He was there for me when I had nobody.”

Her breathing broke painfully.

“So you don’t get to judge him.”

Kyros looked at her silently.

Then finally—

his entire expression changed again.

Cold.

Controlled.

Like he was forcing every emotion back inside.

“Fine.”

His voice became emotionless now.

“If you hate me that much…”

Luna looked away immediately.

“…I won’t come to this company anymore.”

Kyros froze slightly.

Then calmly—

“That’s not possible.”

Luna frowned immediately.

“What?”

Kyros walked a little closer.

“Did you read your contract before signing?”

Luna blinked in confusion.

“You signed a compulsory two-year agreement.”

His tone became professional now.

“So no.”

His eyes stayed on hers.

“You can’t leave.”

Luna stared at him in disbelief.

“You’re threatening me with work now?”

Kyros’s jaw tightened slightly.

“I’m following company policy.”

Those words hurt more than shouting.

Because suddenly—

he sounded like a stranger.

And somehow…

that hurt Luna deeply too.

Without saying another word—

she angrily turned and walked away.

Meanwhile Kyros stood there silently watching her leave.

Again.

Always leaving him behind.

Outside the building—

Luna immediately took out her phone and called Zayn.

No answer.

She frowned slightly.

Again.

Still nothing.

“Pick up…”

Her voice became shaky now.

But Zayn still didn’t answer.
