✦ Chapter Nineteen ✦

Stay Close To Me.

✦

“Okay, madam,” Zayn said dramatically while grabbing his bike keys.

“Are you finally ready or should I come back next year?”

Luna immediately glared at him.

“I was ready ten minutes ago.”

Zayn looked at her from head to toe suspiciously.

“You changed outfits three times.”

“That is called fashion.”

“That is called confusion.”

Luna gasped loudly.

“You’re so rude.”

Zayn grinned.

“And you’re so slow.”

Luna grabbed the nearest pillow and hit his arm immediately.

“Ow! Violence again?”

“You deserve worse.”

Zayn laughed softly before pulling her gently closer by the waist.

“Okay okay, sorry.”

Luna tried acting annoyed dramatically.

“Don’t touch me.”

“But you’re literally standing close to me on purpose.”

Luna immediately looked away.

“Shut up.”

Zayn smirked proudly.

“Caught you.”

“I hate you.”

“No you don’t.”

His voice came softer this time.

Warmer.

And Luna looked away immediately because somehow that simple tone still made her heart flutter a little.

A few minutes later, both of them walked through the crowded evening streets together.

Warm lights glowed everywhere.

Music played softly from nearby shops.

People laughed around them while Luna and Zayn argued about absolutely nothing.

“You walk too fast,” Luna complained.

“You walk too slow.”

“You have long legs!”

“That sounds like a you problem.”

Luna hit his shoulder again.

Zayn laughed harder.

God.

He loved seeing her like this.

Comfortable.

Happy.

Alive.

At one point, Luna suddenly stopped near a small roadside stall.

Her eyes widened immediately.

“Zaynnnn.”

Zayn already sighed.

“No.”

“Please?”

“No.”

“It’s literally just one plushie.”

“You already have fifteen.”

“I need emotional support.”

“You have me.”

The words came out naturally.

Too naturally.

Both of them went silent for one second.

Then Luna slowly smiled.

“Hmmm…”

Zayn narrowed his eyes immediately.

“What does that smile mean?”

“Nothing.”

“That’s a dangerous smile.”

Luna laughed softly before suddenly grabbing his arm and dragging him toward the shop anyway.

“Come on.”

“Luna—”

“Too late.”

Zayn let her pull him forward while shaking his head dramatically.

But honestly?

He didn’t mind.

Not when she looked this happy.

A little later, they sat near the beachside steps eating snacks while the evening breeze hit softly around them.

Luna quietly stole fries from Zayn’s plate.

Zayn immediately looked offended.

“Excuse me?”

Luna blinked innocently.

“What?”

“You said you weren’t hungry.”

“I changed my mind.”

“These are MY fries.”

“Our fries now.”

Zayn stared at her dramatically for two seconds before sighing.

“You’re unbelievable.”

Luna smiled proudly while eating another fry.

“You love me.”

Zayn looked at her immediately.

Too quickly.

Luna noticed and started laughing.

“Oh my God, look at your face.”

“You’re annoying.”

“And you still love me.”

Zayn shook his head while smiling helplessly.

“Unfortunately.”

Luna laughed softly again before leaning her head against his shoulder comfortably.

The city lights reflected beautifully across the water nearby while the evening breeze wrapped around them gently.

For a while, neither of them spoke.

They didn’t need to.

Being together already felt enough.

After a few quiet moments, Zayn slowly looked down at her.

“You know something?”

“Hm?”

“I think this is my favorite version of us.”

Luna looked up curiously.

“What version?”

Zayn smiled softly.

“This one.”

His voice became quieter.

“The one where you smile more.”
