✦ Chapter Twenty-Six ✦

“I Can’t Pretend Anymore.”

✦

Night slowly covered the city while Luna walked out of the company building holding her bag tightly.

The cold breeze hit her softly.

But her thoughts felt louder than everything else.

Kyros.

Again.

Always him.

Luna exhaled shakily trying to calm herself.

She just needed to go home.

That’s it.

But the second she reached the front steps—

she froze.

Because leaning quietly against a black car nearby—

stood Kyros.

Like he had been waiting for her.

Luna’s heartbeat immediately stumbled.

Without even thinking—

she turned slightly trying to walk another way.

But Kyros noticed instantly.

And somehow—

the hurt on his face appeared too quickly.

“Luna.”

Her steps stopped automatically.

God.

Why did his voice affect her like this?

Slowly—

she turned back.

Keeping distance between them carefully.

“We shouldn’t talk.”

Kyros laughed softly.

But it sounded tired.

“You’ve been saying that for days.”

“Because it’s true.”

Kyros looked at her quietly for a few seconds before walking closer.

Not too close.

Just enough for his voice to reach her softly.

“You know what’s pathetic?”

Luna stayed silent.

Kyros smiled weakly to himself.

“Every morning I wake up telling myself I’ll stay away from you.”

His eyes slowly met hers again.

“I keep trying to act normal.”

Something painful moved inside Luna’s chest.

Kyros continued quietly—

“I tried forgetting you.”

The words sounded broken already.

“I tried hating you too.”

Luna’s breath caught slightly.

Kyros looked down briefly before laughing painfully.

“But then you look at me once…”

His voice cracked softly.

“…and suddenly I can’t breathe properly again.”

Silence.

The city sounds around them faded completely.

Because right now—

only Kyros’s voice existed inside Luna’s head.

Luna looked away immediately.

“Please stop…”

But Kyros shook his head softly.

“No.”

For the first time—

his emotions looked completely uncontrollable.

Raw.

Real.

Destroyed.

“You avoid me like I’m nothing to you.”

His eyes slowly filled now.

“But every time you walk away…”

He swallowed hard.

“…it feels like losing you all over again.”

Luna’s fingers trembled slightly.

Because hearing pain in his voice genuinely hurt her too.

Kyros slowly stepped closer.

And this time—

Luna didn’t move away.

“I know you forgot me.”

His voice became quieter now.

“But I didn’t forget you for even one second.”

Luna’s eyes slowly filled.

Kyros stared at her helplessly.

“I can’t do this anymore.”

The tears inside his eyes finally escaped slightly.

And immediately—

Luna froze.

Because seeing Kyros cry felt wrong somehow.

Like someone strong finally breaking apart.

Kyros laughed weakly while wiping his face angrily.

“Look at me…”

His voice cracked again.

“Acting pathetic in the middle of the road because of one girl.”

Luna’s chest hurt painfully now.

Kyros looked directly into her eyes again.

“And the worst part?”

His breathing became uneven.

“…I’d still choose you again.”

Silence.

Complete silence.

Then finally—

Kyros walked closer and suddenly pulled Luna into his arms tightly.

Like he genuinely couldn’t stop himself anymore.

Luna froze instantly.

His embrace felt desperate.

Warm.

Painfully familiar.

Kyros held her like someone terrified of losing the last important thing left in his life.

And quietly—

almost broken—

“I missed you so much.”

Luna’s eyes slowly closed for one second.

Because her heart reacted before her mind could.

But still—

she didn’t hug him back.

She just stood there silently.

Confused.

Overwhelmed.

Emotionally shaking.

And from across the road—

another pair of eyes witnessed everything.

Zayn sat inside his car frozen completely.

His fingers slowly tightened around the steering wheel.

Because from where he sat—

Luna and Kyros looked painfully perfect together.

Like two people who still belonged to each other.

Zayn’s expression broke silently.

Not anger.

Not jealousy.

Just heartbreak.

Pure heartbreak.

Slowly—

before Luna could notice him—

Zayn started the car quietly.

And drove away alone.
