Chapter V

Flat 17

✦

Then from somewhere inside the apartment—

Eris heard breathing.

Slow.

Steady.

Too close.

Her heartbeat instantly lost rhythm.

The hallway suddenly felt freezing cold as she stood outside the slightly open door of Flat 17.

Every instinct screamed at her to walk away.

Go inside apartment 18.

Lock the door.

Pretend none of this was happening.

Instead—

She stayed.

Because curiosity had always been stronger than common sense.

“Hello?” Eris called softly.

Silence answered her.

The darkness inside the apartment remained completely still.

Rain tapped softly against the hallway windows nearby while lights flickered overhead.

Eris swallowed nervously.

“This is how people die in horror movies,” she muttered to herself.

Still—

She stepped closer.

The door opened slightly wider under her hand.

Flat 17 smelled faintly like smoke, rain, and something expensive she couldn’t explain.

The inside looked strange.

Too clean.

Too empty.

Like nobody truly lived there.

Only one dim lamp glowed weakly somewhere deeper inside the apartment.

Eris hesitated near the entrance.

“Okay… if you’re a murderer, this is officially terrifying.”

A low voice suddenly answered from the darkness.

“You talk too much.”

Eris gasped sharply.

Before she could react, someone stepped out from the shadows.

Tall.

Dressed completely in black.

A dark hoodie covered most of his face while dim lighting hid the rest.

But even without seeing him properly—

Eris felt it immediately.

Danger.

The stranger leaned casually against the wall near the hallway inside the apartment.

Watching her silently.

And somehow—

That felt worse than if he had threatened her.

Eris’s pulse quickened painfully.

“You scared me,” she whispered.

A quiet sound escaped him.

Almost amusement.

“You were standing outside my apartment.”

“That’s because your door was open.”

“And yet you still came closer.”

Heat rushed into Eris’s face instantly.

Annoyingly true.

The stranger’s gaze moved slowly over her expression.

Observing.

Careful.

Like he was memorizing every reaction she made.

The silence between them became strangely heavy.

Dangerous.

Eris crossed her arms nervously.

“So… do you normally scare your neighbors for entertainment?”

Another soft almost-laugh came from him.

Then:

“Only the curious ones.”

Her stomach twisted unexpectedly.

God.

Even his voice sounded unfairly attractive.

Eris hated that.

Thunder crashed outside loudly.

For a brief second, hallway light spilled across part of his face.

Not enough to fully see him.

Just enough to notice sharp features and dark eyes watching her intensely beneath the hood.

Then darkness swallowed him again.

Frustration flickered inside her immediately.

She still couldn’t properly see him.

“Do you ever show your face,” she asked carefully, “or is the mysterious psychopath thing permanent?”

Silence.

Then slowly—

He stepped closer.

Eris stopped breathing instantly.

The distance between them disappeared too fast.

Now she could smell rain on his clothes.

Feel warmth radiating from him.

Her heartbeat became embarrassing.

The stranger tilted his head slightly while staring down at her.

“You should stop talking to apartment 15.”

Eris blinked in shock.

“Noah?”

The stranger said nothing.

Which somehow answered everything.

Fear curled slowly through her chest.

“How do you know about Noah?”

Another step closer.

Now her back nearly touched the apartment door behind her.

“You laugh too much around him,” he said quietly.

Eris’s breath caught instantly.

That wasn’t normal jealousy.

That meant—

“You’ve been watching me.”

For the first time, the stranger went silent for too long.

Then finally—

“I watch everyone in this building.”

Lie.

Eris somehow knew it was a lie immediately.

Because the way he looked at her felt too personal.

Too focused.

Too intense.

Thunder crashed again outside.

And this time—

The stranger suddenly reached past her.

Eris flinched slightly when his hand brushed near her waist.

But he only pushed the apartment door shut behind her softly.

Still—

The closeness destroyed her ability to think properly.

“Who are you?” she whispered again.

Silence filled Flat 17.

Then quietly—

“Someone you should stay away from, Eris.”
