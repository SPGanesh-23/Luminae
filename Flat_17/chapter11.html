Chapter 11

Don’t Look At Me Like That

✦

The word hit harder than she expected.

Eris froze instantly.

Something painful twisted inside her chest.

Because somehow—

That hurt more than the attack.

More than the lies.

More than the danger.

A broken laugh escaped her softly.

She nodded once.

Like she understood.

Even though she didn’t.

“Right.”

Adrían’s jaw tightened slightly.

But he said nothing else.

Of course he didn’t.

Eris looked away immediately before he could notice her eyes burning.

Then suddenly—

She stood up from the couch.

Angry.

Embarrassed.

Hurt.

“I’m done trying to understand you.”

Her voice shook slightly despite trying to sound calm.

Adrían remained silent.

That somehow made everything worse.

Eris turned instantly and started walking away.

One step.

Then—

A hand suddenly wrapped around her wrist.

Firm.

Warm.

Desperate.

Eris stopped breathing for a second.

Slowly—

She turned back.

Adrían stood close behind her now.

Too close.

His fingers tightened slightly around her wrist like instinct took control before his mind did.

Like some part of him didn’t want her leaving.

The tension between them became unbearable.

Rain outside.

Heavy silence.

Her heartbeat losing rhythm.

Eris looked down briefly at his hand around her wrist—

Then slowly looked back at him.

Adrían was already staring at her.

Dark eyes unreadable.

But something inside them looked dangerous tonight.

Not violent.

Not cold.

Something worse.

Emotion.

For one brief second—

Eris thought he might finally say what he was really feeling.

Instead—

Reality returned to him.

Adrían’s expression hardened instantly.

Like he remembered who he was.

Slowly—

He let go of her hand.

The warmth disappeared immediately.

Eris’s chest tightened painfully.

Neither of them spoke.

Because suddenly there were too many things neither knew how to say.

Adrían looked away first.

Then without another word—

He turned and walked toward the bedroom down the hallway.

The door shut quietly behind him.

Leaving Eris standing alone in the middle of the safehouse.

Heart racing.

Wrist still burning where he touched her.

And somehow—

That silent pull hurt more than his answer ever did.

Morning sunlight slipped softly through apartment 18.

Eris woke up irritated.

Actually—

More than irritated.

Angry.

Because the first thing she remembered after opening her eyes was Adrían’s voice from last night.

“

“Stay away from me, Eris.”

The words replayed endlessly inside her head the moment she woke up.

Eris groaned angrily into her pillow.

God.

Why did everything Adrían said sound dramatic enough to ruin her entire mood?

She sat up immediately.

Still irritated.

Still hurt.

Still pretending she absolutely did not care.

Which was a lie.

A loud knock suddenly echoed through apartment 18.

Eris frowned instantly.

Another knock came.

Impatient this time.

She rolled her eyes dramatically while walking toward the door.

“What now…”

The moment she unlocked it—

The door opened quickly.

And there he was.

Adrían Noir.

Standing outside apartment 18 like he belonged there.

Black hoodie.

Messy dark hair.

Cold expression.

Unfortunately still attractive.

Eris immediately looked away the second she saw him.

Still angry.

Still refusing to forgive him.

“What do you want?”

Adrían’s eyes stayed on her face silently for a second too long.

Then calmly—

“You forgot your—”

Eris turned immediately in annoyance.

Planning to walk away dramatically.

But the floor near the entrance still had water from last night’s rain.

And before she realized—

Her foot slipped.

“Shi—”

Eris lost balance instantly.

The world tilted dangerously fast.

She squeezed her eyes shut waiting for impact—

But it never came.

Slowly—

She opened her eyes.

Only to realize Adrían had stepped aside instead of catching her.

Eris stared at him in pure betrayal from the floor.

“YOU DIDN’T EVEN TRY TO HELP ME?!”

For one dangerous second—

Adrían looked genuinely close to laughing.

Actually laughing.

Which somehow offended her more.

Eris sat on the floor glaring at him while fixing her messy hair angrily.

“Wow. Gentleman behavior.”

Adrían crouched slightly near her.

Not helping her up.

Of course not.

Instead—

He quietly held out her fallen handkerchief toward her.

Eris blinked.

Then narrowed her eyes suspiciously.

“Seriously?”

Adrían’s lips twitched slightly.

Barely noticeable.

But there.

A smile trying to escape.

And suddenly—

Eris forgot her anger for one second.

Because this was the first time she had seen him look… normal.

Not dangerous.

Not cold.

Just—

A boy trying not to smile.

That somehow felt more dangerous than everything else.

Eris snatched the handkerchief from his hand dramatically.

“I hate you.”

Adrían finally stood back up slowly.

Calm again.

But his eyes still looked lighter somehow.

Then quietly—

“No, you don’t.”

Eris’s heartbeat betrayed her instantly.

Which irritated her even more.

Before she could argue again—

Adrían stepped backward toward Flat 17.

Then without another word—

He closed his apartment door quietly.

Leaving Eris sitting on the floor outside apartment 18.

Completely annoyed.

Completely confused.

And inside Flat 17—

Adrían leaned silently against the closed door.

Then finally—

A soft laugh escaped him.

Small.

Real.

Dangerous.

Because despite everything—

He still thought she looked cute while yelling at him.
