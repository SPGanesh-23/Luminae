Strange Neighbors

✦

The next morning felt painfully normal.

Which honestly annoyed me.

Because after last night’s creepy messages and mysterious window boy situation, I expected at least some dramatic background music when I woke up.

Instead?

Sunlight.

Silence.

And a stiff neck from sleeping sideways.

I sat up slowly and immediately checked my phone.

No new messages.

Good.

Fantastic.

Amazing.

I definitely wasn’t disappointed for half a second.

I quickly threw the phone away before that thought could develop further.

Nope.

Absolutely not.

Thirty minutes later, I was standing in front of my mirror trying to look like someone who totally didn’t move into a haunted apartment building yesterday.

Black jeans.

Grey sweater.

Minimal makeup.

Professional enough to survive the first day at Holloway Publishing.

My phone rang again.

kate .

I answered immediately.

“Good morning, survivor,” Kate said dramatically.

“I’m blocking you.”

“You can’t. I’m your only friend.”

Unfortunately true.

Kate gasped suddenly.

“Oh my god.”

“What now?”

“What if mysterious window boy falls in love with you?”

I deadpanned at my reflection.

“What if I hang up?”

“I’m serious! Imagine the Wattpad potential.”

“He could literally be a murderer.”

Kate paused.

“…Hot murderer?”

I started laughing before I could stop myself.

“You are genuinely the worst person alive.”

“And yet you love me deeply.”

I sighed dramatically. “Tragic honestly.”

Still laughing quietly, I grabbed my bag and headed outside.

The hallway looked less creepy during daytime.

Slightly.

Flat 17 remained completely silent beside mine.

No sounds.

No movement.

Nothing.

I stared at the black door for a second longer than necessary before forcing myself toward the elevator.

The second the doors opened downstairs, an elderly woman nearly walked straight into me.

“Oh dear!” she gasped.

“I’m so sorry—”

“No no, my fault!” I said quickly.

The woman smiled warmly.

Unlike literally everyone else in this building, she looked normal.

Sweet even.

“You’re the new girl from 18, right?”

I blinked slightly. “Yeah.”

“I’m Mrs. Bennett from apartment 12.” She gently touched my arm. “If you ever need food, sugar, life advice, or gossip, my door is always open.”

I smiled instantly.

Okay.

I loved her already.

Before I could reply, another voice suddenly appeared behind us.

“Mrs. Bennett terrifies half the building with her gossip.”

I turned.

A guy around my age leaned casually against the lobby wall holding a coffee cup.

Dark curly hair.

Lazy grin.

Dangerously confident energy.

Mrs. Bennett rolled her eyes affectionately.

“Ignore Noah. He thinks he’s charming.”

“I am charming,” he argued.

Then his eyes shifted toward me.

“You’re apartment 18?”

Something about the question felt slightly strange.

Still, I nodded politely.

“Eris.”

“Noah,” he said easily. “Apartment 15.”

Mrs. Bennett smiled warmly again.

“See? The building isn’t so scary.”

Right.

Because mysterious staring boys and midnight texts were completely normal.

Almost like he heard my thoughts—

My phone buzzed inside my pocket.

My entire body stiffened instantly.

Kate:

Did hot murderer appear again???

I physically relaxed.

God.

I hated this building already.

Noah noticed my expression and laughed softly.

“Rough first night?”

“You have no idea.”

“You get used to the weird noises eventually.”

My smile faded slightly.

“Weird noises?”

Mrs. Bennett immediately smacked his arm.

“Noah.”

“What?” he defended innocently. “The pipes literally scream at night.”

I forced an awkward laugh.

Totally pipes.

Definitely pipes.

By the time I returned home that evening, rain had started again.

The hallway lights flickered softly overhead as I walked toward apartment 18.

Then slowed.

Flat 17.

The black door stood exactly the same.

Silent.

Closed.

But this time—

Something was different.

A light shone faintly beneath the door.

My heartbeat quickened unexpectedly.

So someone really did live there.

I stared for a second too long.

Then suddenly—

The light disappeared.
