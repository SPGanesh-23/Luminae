Apartment 15

✦

By the third day in Ravenfall, I officially realized two things.

One:

Blackthorne Apartments was weird.

And two:

I hated how often I looked at Flat 17.

Even now, while locking my apartment door for work, my eyes drifted there automatically.

Silent.

Closed.

No sounds.

No light beneath the door.

Still…

Something about it pulled my attention every single time.

“Careful.”

I jumped violently at the sudden voice behind me.

A laugh followed immediately.

“Wow. You scare easily.”

I turned sharply.

Noah leaned casually against the hallway wall near the elevator, looking far too entertained.

“You almost gave me a heart attack,”

I muttered.

“That’s dramatic.”

“You appeared out of nowhere like a serial killer.”

“That hurts,” he said, placing a hand over his chest. “I at least look like a charming serial killer.”

I accidentally laughed.

Great.

Now he looked proud of himself.

“See? We’re bonding already.”

“I don’t even know your last name.”

“Good point.” He held out his hand dramatically. “Noah Carter. Apartment 15. Professional annoyance.”

I shook my head while pressing the elevator button.

“Eris Vale. Apartment 18. Tired constantly.”

“Nice to meet you, Tired Constantly.”

The elevator doors opened slowly.

We stepped inside together.

For a moment, silence settled comfortably between us.

Honestly?

Noah was easy to talk to.

Too easy.

“So,” he said casually, “how’s your first week in Blackthorne?”

I considered lying.

Then remembered the creepy messages.

“The building feels haunted.”

Noah laughed softly.

“That’s actually the nicest thing anyone’s said about it.”

“I’m serious.”

“I know.”

The elevator creaked slightly as it moved downward.

Then Noah suddenly glanced toward me.

“You should avoid apartment 17 though.”

My stomach tightened instantly.

I tried keeping my expression normal.

“Why?”

He shrugged.

“No reason.”

“That sounds suspicious.”

“Everything in this building is suspicious.”

Fair point.

Still…

Something about the way he said it bothered me.

Careful.

Avoid apartment 17.

Almost like a warning.

I looked at him carefully.

“So someone really lives there?”

For the first time since meeting him, Noah hesitated.

Only for a second.

But I noticed.

Then he looked back at me.

“Did you see someone?”

The question came too quickly.

Too directly.

A weird feeling settled in my chest.

“Maybe.”

Noah stared at me quietly for a moment before looking away again.

“Hm.”

That was it.

No explanation.

No joke.

Just that single response.

The elevator doors opened downstairs before I could ask anything else.

Noah walked backward toward the lobby entrance with his hands inside his pockets.

“Anyway,” he said casually, “if you get murdered, text me first.”

I rolled my eyes automatically.

“You’re unbelievably annoying.”

“And yet you smiled.”

Unfortunately true.

By evening, rain had covered Ravenfall again.

Apparently this city was physically incapable of having normal weather.

I entered Blackthorne Apartments exhausted after work.

The lobby felt quieter than usual tonight.

The old manager barely looked up from his newspaper.

Even the elevator seemed slower.

Something about the building felt… heavier.

Like the air itself knew something I didn’t.

The hallway lights flickered softly when I reached the eighth floor.

Apartment 18.

Flat 17.

My footsteps slowed automatically.

Because tonight—

The door to Flat 17 was slightly open.

Just enough to reveal darkness inside.

My pulse quickened instantly.

Every instinct told me to ignore it.

Walk away.

Go inside my apartment.

Instead—

I stared.

The hallway suddenly felt freezing cold.

Completely silent.

Then from somewhere inside the apartment—

I heard breathing.
