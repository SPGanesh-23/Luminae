Chapter X

You're My Weakness

✦

The lobby fell completely silent after Adrían’s threat.

Rain hammered violently outside the glass doors while Lucien remained pinned against the wall.

And somehow—

The terrifying part wasn’t Adrían.

It was Lucien smiling through it.

Like this amused him.

Like he had just confirmed something important.

Slowly, Lucien looked past Adrían’s shoulder toward Eris.

That smile never disappeared.

“Interesting.”

Adrían’s grip tightened instantly.

“Stop looking at her.”

Cold.

Sharp.

Possessive.

Lucien only chuckled softly.

“You disappeared for months,” he said calmly. “And this is what finally breaks your control?”

Eris frowned immediately.

What did that mean?

Adrían’s jaw tightened dangerously.

For the first time—

He actually looked close to losing control.

Lucien noticed too.

Which only made his expression darker with amusement.

Then quietly—

Too quietly—

“That girl is your weakness now.”

Eris’s heartbeat stumbled painfully.

Adrían shoved Lucien away immediately.

Hard.

“Leave.”

Lucien straightened his jacket slowly.

Completely calm again.

Then his eyes moved toward Eris one final time.

“Be careful who you trust, Miss Eris.”

Something about his tone made fear crawl slowly down her spine.

Then Lucien walked out of Blackthorne Apartments without another word.

The black car outside disappeared into the rain seconds later.

Silence returned.

Heavy.

Uncomfortable.

Eris slowly looked toward Adrían.

“What the hell was that?”

No answer.

Adrían stared toward the rain-covered entrance silently.

Expression unreadable again.

Like the violent moment earlier never happened.

Eris stepped closer angrily.

“No seriously, who IS he?”

Still nothing.

God.

This man was impossible.

“You threatened to kill someone!”

Finally—

Adrían looked at her.

Dark eyes steady.

Cold.

“I meant it.”

The honesty in his voice scared her instantly.

Not dramatic.

Not emotional.

Just fact.

Eris crossed her arms tightly.

“You don’t get to say terrifying things and then go silent!”

Adrían rubbed tiredly across his jaw before walking toward the stairwell.

“Go upstairs, Eris.”

“Oh no,” she snapped immediately while following him. “You are NOT disappearing again after whatever THAT was.”

To her surprise—

He didn’t stop her.

The hallway outside Flat 17 felt colder than usual.

Lights flickered weakly overhead while thunder shook the building softly.

Adrían unlocked his apartment door silently.

Eris noticed blood dripping faintly from his knuckles now.

Her anger disappeared instantly.

“You’re hurt.”

“It’s nothing.”

“That is literally blood.”

Adrían barely looked at his hand.

Like pain didn’t matter anymore.

That bothered her more than it should have.

Without thinking, Eris grabbed his wrist before he could enter the apartment.

The second she touched him—

Adrían froze.

Completely.

His eyes slowly moved toward her hand around his wrist.

The atmosphere shifted immediately.

Too intense.

Too aware.

Eris swallowed nervously but forced herself to continue.

“You need to clean that.”

“I’ve had worse.”

“Congratulations. Still cleaning it.”

For one long second—

Adrían simply stared at her.

Then quietly:

“You should stop doing that.”

Eris frowned slightly.

“Doing what?”

“Acting like I’m safe to be around.”

The low warning in his voice sent chills down her spine.

But somehow—

She didn’t let go.

Bad decision probably.

Still—

She pulled him toward apartment 18 anyway.

“Sit down before you bleed on my floor.”

Shockingly—

Adrían listened.

A few minutes later, silence filled Eris’s kitchen.

Rain tapped softly against the windows while she cleaned blood from Adrían’s knuckles carefully.

The entire situation felt unreal.

Adrían Noir—

missing billionaire heir—

sat inside her apartment while she patched up his injuries like this was normal.

His gaze never left her face once.

Not once.

It made concentrating impossible.

Finally, Eris sighed softly.

“You’re staring.”

“You’re nervous.”

“That’s because you threatened murder an hour ago.”

A faint almost-smirk appeared briefly near his mouth.

Gone too quickly.

Eris hated how unfairly attractive that looked.

“You still didn’t answer me,” she muttered while wrapping bandages around his hand carefully. “Who was Lucien?”

Silence.

Then finally—

“Someone my father trusts.”

“And your father sent him after you?”

Adrían leaned back slightly in the chair.

Dark eyes unreadable again.

“My family doesn’t like losing control of things.”

The way he said things instead of people made unease twist inside her chest.

Eris slowly looked up at him.

“Why are they searching for you?”

Long silence.

Thunder crashed outside softly.

Then quietly—

“Because I left.”

That wasn’t the full truth.

She knew it immediately.

But before she could push further—

Adrían suddenly reached toward her face.

Eris froze instantly.

His fingers brushed lightly near her cheek.

Slow.

Careful.

Her heartbeat lost rhythm immediately.

Adrían’s eyes darkened slightly.

Then he pulled away holding something small between his fingers.

A tiny black device.

Eris stared in confusion.

“What is that?”

Adrían’s expression turned terrifyingly cold.

“Tracker.”

Fear hit instantly.

“What?”

His jaw tightened.

“Lucien put it on you downstairs.”

Eris stopped breathing.

Suddenly every part of the apartment felt unsafe.

Adrían crushed the tracker easily inside his hand.

Then his eyes lifted toward her again.

Darker now.

Protective.

Dangerous.

“They know about you now.”

Silence filled the kitchen.

Eris’s pulse pounded painfully.

Slowly—

She whispered:

“Can I trust you?”

Adrían stared at her for a long moment.

Then quietly—

“No.”
