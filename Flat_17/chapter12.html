Chapter 12

Carry Me Then

✦

For the next two days—

Eris completely ignored Flat 17.

Or at least…

she tried to.

Every time she stepped outside apartment 18, she acted like Adrían didn’t exist.

If the elevator opened and he was inside?

She walked away.

If he looked at her?

She looked somewhere else dramatically.

If he spoke?

Ignored.

Completely.

Which honestly started becoming funny for Adrían very quickly.

Because Eris was terrible at pretending not to care.

That morning—

Eris stood stubbornly near the staircase entrance with grocery bags hanging from both hands.

The elevator doors remained open beside her.

Adrían stood inside silently waiting.

“Get in.”

Eris immediately shook her head.

“No.”

Adrían’s expression stayed completely calm.

“Eris.”

“I’m not getting into an elevator with you.”

“Why?”

She looked away dramatically.

“Because I’m still angry.”

A tiny smile almost appeared on Adrían’s face again.

God.

She was impossible.

Eris adjusted the heavy bags angrily before starting toward the stairs instead.

“I’ll use this.”

Adrían stared at her in disbelief.

“You live on the eighth floor.”

“I know how numbers work.”

Then she started climbing stubbornly.

One floor.

Two floors.

By the third—

Eris’s breathing became dramatic enough for Adrían to look genuinely concerned.

Still—

She refused help.

Of course she did.

“I’m perfectly fine.”

She absolutely was not.

Adrían watched her struggle silently for another few seconds before finally walking toward her.

Eris narrowed her eyes immediately.

“Don’t.”

Too late.

In one smooth movement—

Adrían grabbed all the grocery bags with one hand…

And lifted Eris into his arms with the other.

Eris gasped instantly.

“ADRÍAN?!”

Her heartbeat completely lost rhythm.

Adrían continued climbing the stairs calmly like carrying her weighed nothing.

Meanwhile—

Eris internally stopped functioning.

“PUT ME DOWN.”

“No.”

“I hate you.”

“You already said that.”

Eris glared at him immediately.

Unfortunately—

That became difficult because of how close his face suddenly was.

His jawline.

Dark eyes.

Messy hair falling slightly over his forehead.

Unfairly beautiful.

God.

This man was genuinely dangerous to women’s mental stability.

By the fifth floor—

Eris stopped yelling.

Mostly because Adrían’s hand resting securely against her waist kept distracting her.

The staircase became strangely quiet afterward.

Only footsteps echoing softly.

Then suddenly—

“Adrían.”

“Mhm?”

“Put me down now.”

But this time her voice sounded quieter.

Less angry.

Adrían looked down at her immediately.

And unfortunately—

That was a mistake.

Because Eris was already staring at him.

Their eyes locked instantly.

Everything slowed.

The tension between them became unbearable again.

No teasing.

No fighting.

Just silence.

Heavy breathing.

His arms around her.

Her fingers lightly gripping his hoodie without realizing.

For one dangerous second—

Neither of them looked away.

And suddenly—

Eris forgot why she was angry in the first place.

Adrían’s expression softened slightly while looking at her.

Not cold anymore.

Not distant.

Just—

Him.

That terrified Eris more than anything.

Then quietly—

Adrían broke the silence first.

“Apartment 18.”

Eris blinked quickly like waking from a trance.

Oh my God.

They were already there.

Reality returned instantly.

And unfortunately—

So did her dramatic personality.

Eris immediately pushed herself out of his arms and fixed her hair angrily.

“You could’ve just carried the bags normally, psycho.”

Adrían looked dangerously close to smiling again.

“You were slower than the bags.”

“RUDE.”

Eris unlocked apartment 18 dramatically while muttering insults under her breath.

Then stormed inside.

But before she could close the door—

A hand stopped it gently.

Eris looked up instantly.

Adrían stepped inside quietly behind her.

The apartment suddenly felt way too small.

Way too warm.

“Why are you inside my apartment?”

Adrían placed the grocery bags on the kitchen counter calmly.

“You’d probably drop them again.”

Eris gasped in betrayal.

“That happened ONCE.”

Adrían finally smirked slightly.

Tiny.

Dangerous.

Beautiful.

Eris’s heartbeat betrayed her immediately.

God.

This man needed to stop doing that.

The silence between them suddenly changed.

Softer now.

Closer.

Adrían slowly stepped toward her again.

Not threatening.

Not cold.

Just close enough for her breathing to become uneven.

Eris stayed completely still.

Which was honestly concerning.

Because normally she would’ve yelled by now.

Instead—

Her eyes kept falling toward his lips accidentally.

And judging by the way Adrían’s gaze darkened slightly—

He noticed.
