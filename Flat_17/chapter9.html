Chapter IX

Don’t Look Back

✦

For the first time since meeting him—

Adrian actually sounded angry.

That scared Eris more than anything else.

Because until now, he had always been calm.

Controlled.

Like nothing affected him.

But this?

This felt different.

Dangerous.

Eris stared at him for one frozen second while rain thundered outside the lobby windows.

The stranger near the entrance still watched them silently.

Waiting.

Then Adrian stepped slightly closer to her.

Blocking the man’s view completely.

“Go upstairs, Eris.”

Low.

Sharp.

Not a request anymore.

Her heartbeat stumbled painfully.

“What’s happening?” she whispered.

No answer.

Adrian never looked away from the stranger.

The atmosphere inside the lobby tightened heavily.

Like something terrible was seconds away from happening.

Eris swallowed hard.

“Adrian—”

“Don’t argue with me right now.”

The coldness in his voice shocked her instantly.

Fear curled through her chest.

Not because she thought he would hurt her—

But because he genuinely sounded worried.

For her.

The realization confused her even more.

Slowly, Eris turned toward the stairwell.

But before leaving—

She glanced back once.

Big mistake.

The stranger smiled at her again.

Small.

Calm.

Wrong.

Then quietly—

He spoke for the first time.

“You should stay away from dangerous boys, Miss Eris.”

Ice slid down her spine immediately.

How did he know my name?

Eris stopped breathing for a second.

Adrían’s entire expression darkened instantly.

Something terrifying flashed through his eyes.

The stranger noticed too.

But instead of fear—

He looked amused.

That somehow made everything worse.

Then Adrían took one slow step toward him.

And the entire lobby suddenly felt deadly quiet.

“You shouldn’t have come here, Lucien.”

Lucien.

The stranger smiled slightly wider.

“Your father disagrees.”

Eris frowned immediately.

Father?

What the hell was happening?

Lucien’s gaze moved toward her briefly again.

“You’ve created a problem for yourself, Adrian.”

Wrong move.

Very wrong move.

Because the second Lucien looked at her—

Adrian completely lost the last bit of calmness left in him.

The air itself felt dangerous now.

Cold.

Violent.

Protective.

“Don’t look at her.”

The warning came out terrifyingly quiet.

Lucien raised an eyebrow slowly.

Interesting.

Like he had just discovered something important.

Something about Eris.

Oh no.

Adrían realized it too.

And somehow—

That scared him.

Eris saw it clearly for the first time.

Not fear for himself.

Fear for her.

Lucien smiled knowingly.

Then softly—

“Now I understand.”

Adrian moved instantly.

Fast enough to make Eris gasp.

One second he stood beside her.

The next—

He had grabbed Lucien violently by the collar and slammed him hard against the lobby wall.

The sound echoed brutally through the building.

“Adrian!” Eris shouted in shock.

Lucien barely reacted.

Which somehow made him scarier.

Even pinned against the wall—

He looked calm.

Amused even.

Rain hammered violently outside while the two men stared at each other like enemies who already knew how dangerous the other could become.

Then Adrian spoke quietly near Lucien’s face.

“If you involve her in this… I’ll kill you.”

Eris’s heartbeat stopped completely.
