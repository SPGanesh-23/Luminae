Chapter 13

You Lied To Me

✦

Weeks changed everything.

Somehow—

Without either of them realizing—

Apartment 17 stopped feeling lonely.

Eris became part of Adrían’s life slowly.

Dangerously.

Morning coffees.

Late-night arguments.

Her random laughter filling Flat 17.

His quiet stares when she wasn’t looking.

It happened naturally.

And that terrified Adrían more than bullets ever could.

Because for the first time in years—

He felt happy.

Real happy.

Rain tapped softly against the windows of Flat 17 that night.

Warm lights filled the apartment quietly.

Eris sat on Adrían’s kitchen counter eating noodles dramatically while complaining about her day.

“And then Kate said I have anger issues.”

Adrían leaned against the counter beside her silently.

Watching her.

Again.

Always watching her.

Eris narrowed her eyes immediately.

“Why are you staring at me?”

A tiny smile appeared on his face.

Small.

Rare.

Real.

“You talk too much.”

“Yet you keep listening.”

Silence.

Then softly—

“Only to you.”

Eris’s heartbeat instantly betrayed her.

God.

This man seriously needed warning labels.

She tried hiding her smile by looking away.

But Adrían noticed anyway.

Of course he did.

Slowly—

He stepped closer to her legs .

Too close.

The atmosphere changed immediately.

Warm.

Dangerous.

Eris looked up at him quietly now.

No teasing.

No fighting.

Just him.

Adrían’s hand moved slowly toward her face.

Brushing a strand of hair behind her ear gently.

A touch so soft it almost hurt.

“You’re staring again,”

Eris whispered.

His dark eyes stayed fixed on hers.

“Can you blame me?”

Her breath caught instantly.

The tension became unbearable.

Then slowly—

Adrían leaned closer.

And this time—

Eris didn’t pull away.

Their lips almost touched—

Suddenly—

BANG.

The apartment door exploded open violently.

Eris flinched instantly.

Adrían turned immediately.

Gun already in his hand within seconds.

Men stormed inside the apartment.

Police.

Armed officers surrounded Flat 17 instantly.

“DROP THE WEAPON!”

Everything happened too fast.

Adrían’s eyes darkened dangerously while pulling Eris behind him protectively.

Instinct.

Always instinct.

But then—

He froze.

Because the officers weren’t aiming at Eris.

They were aiming at him.

The atmosphere changed instantly.

Heavy silence filled the apartment.

Then slowly—

Adrían looked back at Eris.

Confusion crossed his face for the first time.

Because she wasn’t reacting.

Wasn’t scared.

Wasn’t surprised enough.

Eris looked shaken.

Conflicted.

Like something inside her was breaking.

Then slowly—

She stepped away from behind him.

Adrían’s heartbeat stopped.

“Eris?”

Her eyes met his briefly.

Pain flashed across her face.

Real pain.

But then—

She walked toward the officers.

And stood beside them.

One officer handed her a pair of handcuffs quietly.

Adrían stared at her in complete disbelief.

No.

No no no—

The realization hit violently.

His breathing slowed dangerously.

The room suddenly felt colder.

Like the world stopped moving.

“You…”

His voice came out broken.

Eris couldn’t look at him now.

Because guilt was already destroying her.

Adrían laughed once softly.

But it sounded empty.

Destroyed.

“You set me up.”

Silence.

Nobody moved.

Nobody spoke.

Because the heartbreak inside Adrían’s eyes was genuinely painful to watch.

Eris finally forced herself to speak quietly.

“Adrían…”

Wrong choice.

Because hearing his name from her suddenly made everything worse.

His jaw tightened violently.

The hurt slowly transformed into rage.

Not explosive rage.

The dangerous kind.

Cold rage.

“Was any of it real?”

Eris’s eyes filled instantly.

Because THAT question hurt the most.

And the terrifying part?

She didn’t know how to answer anymore.

Police slowly moved toward Adrían carefully.

Even armed officers looked nervous around him.

Because despite standing still—

Adrían Noir looked terrifying now.

His eyes never left Eris.

Not once.

Then finally—

He spoke quietly.

Low voice.

Dead calm.

Which somehow felt more threatening than shouting.

“You betrayed me.”

Tears burned inside Eris’s eyes immediately.

But she stayed silent.

Because she had no defense left.

Adrían slowly held his hands out while officers cuffed him carefully.

Still staring at her.

Still looking destroyed.

Then just before they dragged him away—

He leaned slightly closer toward Eris.

And whispered:

“I’ll come back for you.”

Her breathing stopped instantly.

Adrían’s dark eyes locked onto hers one final time.

Cold.

Heartbroken.

Obsessive.

Dangerous.

“And when I do…”

A pause.

Pure rage hidden beneath calmness.

“You’re going to regret this, Eris.”

Then police dragged him out of Flat 17.

Leaving Eris standing alone in the destroyed apartment.

Heart shattering silently—

While the man she loved disappeared in handcuffs because of her.
