Chapter 14

I Came Back For You

✦

Three years later.

Ravenfall changed.

But Eris didn’t.

Not really.

She still lived alone.

Still checked shadows too carefully.

Still woke up from nightmares with Adrían’s voice inside her head.

“You’re going to regret this, Eris.”

Three years…

And she still remembered the way he looked at her when police dragged him away.

Broken.

Betrayed.

Dangerous.

The Noir case slowly disappeared from headlines after that.

Leon Noir died one year later.

Most of the family business collapsed.

And Adrían Noir?

Stayed locked away.

At least—

That’s what Eris kept telling herself.

Rain echoed softly outside her apartment that night.

The city looked dark again.

Too quiet.

Eris sat curled on the couch scrolling mindlessly through her phone while trying to ignore the strange feeling creeping through her chest.

Like someone was watching her.

Again.

She frowned slightly.

The apartment suddenly felt colder.

Slowly—

She looked toward the hallway.

Nothing.

Silence.

But the feeling remained.

Heavy.

Wrong.

Eris stood up carefully.

“Hello?”

No response.

Her heartbeat quickened instantly.

She laughed nervously to herself.

“Okay… maybe I’m finally losing my mind.”

Then—

A soft sound echoed somewhere inside the apartment.

Not outside.

Inside.

Eris froze instantly.

Her breathing stopped.

The kitchen light flickered softly.

Shadows moved across the dark apartment walls.

Fear crawled painfully through her chest now.

Someone was there.

She could feel it.

Eris slowly stepped backward toward the living room table where her phone rested.

Then suddenly—

The television turned on by itself.

Eris gasped sharply.

Breaking news flashed across the screen immediately.

“Adrían Noir officially released this evening following the reopening of the Noir investigation—”

Eris stopped breathing.

The TV showed black luxury cars surrounded by cameras.

Reporters shouting.

Police struggling to control crowds.

And then—

Adrían stepped out onto the screen.

Black suit.

Older now.

Sharper.

Colder.

Dark eyes hidden beneath shadows.

But still him.

Still terrifyingly beautiful.

Still the man she never truly escaped.

Fear twisted violently inside her chest.

No.

No no no—

That wasn’t possible.

Her hands started shaking instantly.

Then slowly—

A voice echoed behind her.

Low.

Calm.

Familiar enough to destroy her heartbeat instantly.

“Miss me?”

Eris froze completely.

Every breath disappeared from her lungs.

Slowly—

Terrified—

She turned around.

And there he was.

Standing silently in the darkness behind her.

Adrían Noir.

Black coat.

Hands inside pockets.

Sharp eyes locked onto her like he’d never stopped looking.

The apartment suddenly felt too small.

Too cold.

Too dangerous.

Eris’s lips parted slightly in shock.

“Adrían…”

A small smile appeared on his face.

Not warm.

Not soft.

Obsessive.

Broken.

Terrifying.

Then quietly—

He tilted his head slightly while staring at her.

“I told you I’d come back for you, Eris.”

“If I can’t forget you…
Then I’ll ruin you instead.”

— THE END —
