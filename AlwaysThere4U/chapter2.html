1:13 AM — somewhere in Westwood

The phone screen was the only light in the room.

Outside, rain tapped against the window in uneven rhythms.

The main account was still open from earlier — notifications still coming in, still being ignored.

It was easier to close it.

Open the other one instead.

The account nobody knew about. No name. No picture. Nothing that could be traced back.

just.a.nobody00.

It had been made three weeks ago during a bad night. Created in about forty seconds. Followed one account and nothing else.

AlwaysThere4U.

The latest post was still on screen.

"Some people only text when they're lonely.
Some text because they thought of you first.
There's a difference."

Read it twice.

Then opened the DM window.

Stared at the blinking cursor for almost a full minute.

Typed.

Deleted it.

Typed again.

Sent it before overthinking.

1:13 AM — Ethan's room

Ethan stared at the message from just.a.nobody00 for a long second.

No profile picture. No followers. No posts. A completely hollow account.

But the question inside it wasn't hollow at all.

how do you stop feeling lonely even when you're surrounded by people all day?

Rain tapped softly against the apartment windows.

Ethan read it once more. Then slowly typed:

sometimes being surrounded just means there are people nearby.
it doesn't always mean someone sees you.
Typing bubbles appeared almost instantly. Disappeared. Appeared again.
wow.
okay that hit immediately 😭

Ethan leaned back slightly in his chair.

Outside, thunder rolled quietly through the city.

Another message arrived.

idk.
i think something might actually be wrong with me

Ethan frowned a little.

why would you think that?
Three dots. Pause. Three dots again.
because i can literally walk into a room full of people who like me and still feel weirdly alone

He read the sentence twice.

He got versions of this question regularly. But something about the phrasing — a room full of people who like me — felt specific. Tired. Like whoever this was had been sitting with that feeling for a long time.

He typed carefully.

maybe being liked and being understood aren't the same thing.
Seen immediately. Long pause.
wait.
hold on.
that's actually exactly it

Ethan rubbed tiredly at one eye.

His clock read 1:26 AM.

He should sleep.

Instead he stayed.

Because somehow this conversation felt different from the others tonight. Less like someone reaching for comfort. More like someone reaching for the right words they hadn't been able to find on their own.

Another message arrived.

everyone talks TO me all day
but nobody really talks WITH me if that makes sense
it makes sense.
you reply insanely fast btw

A tiny smile tugged at Ethan's mouth.

insomnia gives you hobbies.
😭 fair

For a few seconds the chat stayed quiet.

Then another message popped up unexpectedly.

can i ask you something kinda mean?

Ethan blinked.

depends how mean
are you secretly like 45 years old and divorced

He laughed. Actually laughed out loud this time. The sound surprised even him.

no.
emotionally exhausted maybe.
divorced no
that sounds exactly like something a divorced person would say

Another message came immediately after.

seriously though.
how are you so good at talking to people?

Ethan stared at the question quietly.

The truth sat somewhere ugly in the back of his mind.

Because he spent most of his life invisible. Invisible people notice everything.

Instead he typed:

i listen more than most people do.
Seen. Typing…
that's rare

Before Ethan could answer, another message appeared.

sorry if i'm bothering you btw

His eyebrows pulled together slightly.

Whoever this was — blank account, no trace of who they were — they were still worried about being too much for a stranger online.

you're not bothering me.

He sent it before overthinking.

The typing bubbles stopped for a moment after that.

Then:

thanks.

Short message. Simple.

But it felt strangely honest.

Ethan glanced at the username again. just.a.nobody00. No posts. No followers. Nothing.

Whoever was on the other side of this had gone through the effort of creating an account nobody could trace back to them just to ask this question.

That said something.

He just didn't know what yet.

WAIT can i ask something random
go ahead
how are you this easy to talk to

Ethan stared at the message for a second.

what do you mean
like…
i don't even tell my actual friends half this stuff 😭

He leaned back slightly in his chair.

Another message came before he could reply.

usually when people get close to you they start using things against you later
or they screenshot everything

Ethan's expression softened a little. Slowly, he typed:

people tell me things because they know i'm safe with them.
okay wow, I too feel the same

Ethan looked at the rain sliding down his window. Then typed:

i think people just want one person they don't have to perform around.
The typing bubbles stopped for a moment.
YES.
exactly that.
talking to you feels weirdly peaceful

At 2:03 AM, the other person suddenly sent:

wait.
what school do you even go to?

Ethan froze.

His fingers stopped moving instantly.

The rain outside suddenly sounded louder.

He stared at the blinking cursor.

Too personal.

Too close.

He typed carefully:

some school in westwood.

Technically true.

oh wait seriously? same

Ethan blinked at the screen.

He sat up slightly in his chair.

Same school. Same town. And he had absolutely no idea who this was.

westwood high is emotionally cursed
REAL.

Another pause. Then:

what year are you in?

Ethan hesitated.

Dangerous question.

But technically harmless.

junior.
WAIT ME TOO

Ethan closed his eyes briefly.

Same school. Same year. Westwood High had roughly two hundred juniors. That meant whoever this was — he'd probably walked past them today.

Could be anyone.

He had no way of knowing.

small world
okay now i'm curious if i've seen you before

Ethan stared at that sentence.

Somewhere across Westwood, a junior he'd never identified was wondering the same thing about him.

He typed:

probably.
i blend into crowds pretty well
that's kinda sad

He looked at the message for a second. Then typed:

sometimes peaceful too.
Seen.

Typing bubbles appeared slowly this time.

i don't think i'd survive being invisible honestly

Ethan glanced toward the dark reflection in his monitor.

Somewhere at Westwood High, someone believed that. Was probably right, too, given how they'd described their life — rooms full of people, constant attention, never quite seen.

Funny. That actually sounded exhausting in its own way.

i should probably sleep
probably.
thanks for talking to me tonight though.
genuinely.

Something about that word hit differently.

Genuinely.

No performance.

No audience.

Just honesty at 2 AM from someone he'd never be able to pick out of a crowd.

Ethan typed slowly.

get some sleep, nobody.

He'd meant it as a joke — the username. A callback.

Three dots appeared one last time.

that's the first time being called nobody felt kinda nice

Then:

goodnight always there for you

Offline.

The screen went still.

Ethan sat motionless for a moment.

Then slowly looked up toward the rain-covered windows.

Tomorrow morning he'd walk into Westwood High's hallways filled with two hundred juniors.

One of them had just told him things they didn't tell their real friends.

And he had absolutely no idea which one.

That thought stayed with him longer than it should have.
