Invisible IRL

✦

Monday mornings made everyone louder.

Lockers slammed shut.

Someone was skateboarding through the hallway even though it was definitely not allowed.

Someone yelled about a football game. Someone else laughed loud enough for three classrooms to hear.

And in the middle of all that noise, Ethan Parker walked through the hallway unnoticed.

Gray hoodie under his school jacket. Headphones around his neck. One hand in pocket.

Nobody stopped him.

Nobody called his name.

A few people moved aside automatically without really looking at who they were making space for.

That was normal.

Ethan wasn't bullied. Wasn't hated.

People just… forgot him easily.

He reached Classroom 214 and quietly stepped inside.

The room was already chaos.

"Bro, she literally blocked me."

"That's insane."

Laughter got silenced near the center of the room.

Ethan looked up instinctively.

Of course.

Chloe Hopper.

Even sitting down, she somehow became the center of the classroom naturally.

Phone in hand with Instagram still open.

10.4K followers.

Not influencer-famous.

School-famous.

The kind of famous where freshmen whispered when she walked by, athletes flirted with her constantly, and random guys suddenly started caring about poetry if she mentioned liking poetry once.

And somehow, Chloe looked bored by all of it.

A tall guy from the basketball team leaned against her desk.

"So are you coming Friday or not?"

Chloe barely looked up from her phone. "Depends."

"On what?"

"If I decide I like people by then."

The class laughed instantly.

The guy grinned anyway like rejection from her still counted as attention.

"You reject everyone," Claire muttered beside her.

Chloe finally looked up. "Because everyone talks like they learned flirting from TikTok."

"That is NOT true."

"No. That's how everyone is. No one is literally my TYPE here."

The classroom exploded.

Even Mr. Bennett laughed while entering.

"Good morning, emotionally unstable people."

"Morning, Mr. Bennett," the class replied unevenly.

Ethan quietly moved toward his usual seat near the windows while conversations continued around him like background noise.

As he sat down, Chloe glanced across the room briefly.

Her eyes passed over Ethan for less than a second.

Then moved on.

Not rude.

Not intentional.

Just automatic.

Like looking past a stranger at a bus stop.

Ethan looked away toward the rain outside.

The lecture started.

Pens scratched paper.

Someone whispered through the entire lesson.

Mr. Bennett threatened to quit teaching twice before first period even ended.

And slowly, naturally, Ethan disappeared into another ordinary school day.

By 7:12 PM, rain hammered against Ethan's apartment windows hard enough to shake them slightly.

The apartment was empty when he got home.

Again.

His mom had texted earlier:

double shift tonight. food's in the fridge ❤️

Ethan microwaved leftover pasta, carried it upstairs, and dropped into the chair beside his desk.

For a moment, the room stayed silent except for thunder outside.

Then he turned on his monitor.

Everything changed instantly.

Notifications flooded the screen.

Instagram DMs. Comments. Mentions. Unread requests.

Hundreds of them.

At the center of the screen sat the account name:

AlwaysThere4U

Followers: 104,823

The blue glow from the monitor reflected softly across Ethan's tired face.

Funny.

At school, nobody waited for him.

Online, people searched for him every night.

His newest post filled the screen:

"Some people only text when they're lonely. Some text because they thought of you first. There's a difference."

61,000 likes.

Thousands of comments.

He opened the first unread message.

hey.
are you awake?

Ethan typed calmly.

yeah.
what happened?
Typing bubbles appeared instantly.

His followers always trusted him more than real people.

I think my friends only tolerate me because I'm useful to them

Ethan leaned back slightly. Then typed:

people who only value usefulness usually disappear the second you stop giving.
the ones worth keeping stay even during the quiet versions of you.
Seen. Typing…
how do you always know exactly what to say

Ethan stared at the question quietly.

Because honestly?

He didn't.

He just spent most of his life observing people while they overlooked him.

Eventually you start noticing things: fake laughs, forced confidence, sadness hidden behind "lol."

Still, he replied:

maybe lonely people become good at understanding loneliness.
Three dots appeared again.
Wow.
that sounded kinda sad 😭

A small smile crossed Ethan's face.

Tiny. Brief.

Gone a second later.

More messages arrived.

A breakup.

he said I was "too much" and then just… left
too much how?
too emotional. too needy. too everything apparently
idk maybe he was right

Ethan exhaled slowly.

someone calling you "too much" usually means they weren't enough.
what?
people who can actually handle you don't use that phrase. they just… stay.
Long pause. Typing bubbles appeared, disappeared, appeared again.
i never thought about it that way
you okay right now?
not really
not really is honest. that's more than most people manage tonight.

Then a girl showing him pictures of a stray dog she rescued.

OKAY I NEED TO SHOW SOMEONE THIS RIGHT NOW

Three photos loaded slowly.

A small dog. Muddy. One ear flopped sideways like it hadn't figured out ears yet. Sitting in what looked like a laundromat, wrapped in someone's jacket.

i found him outside the convenience store. he was just sitting in the rain looking confused about everything
i named him Biscuit
you named him already?
i named him in the first thirty seconds yes
so he's yours now
my landlord is going to actually kill me but YES he's mine now. look at his little face. LOOK

Ethan looked. He typed:

he does look like a Biscuit.
RIGHT??? he has biscuit energy
are you keeping him warm
i bought him a hot dog from the gas station and he ate it in like 0.4 seconds and now he's asleep on my foot and i genuinely cannot move and i don't even care

Ethan smiled at that.

Actually smiled.

Not the small, brief kind.

The kind that stayed.

A guy terrified of disappointing his parents.

i got a B+ on the exam
that's solid
not for my dad it isn't
what does he say?
nothing. that's worse. he just goes quiet and nods and then asks if i studied enough. and i did. i studied every night for two weeks.
and the B+ still felt like failing?
it felt like i failed HIM. which is different.

Ethan sat with that for a second.

yeah. that one's harder.
does it get easier?

He typed honestly:

i don't know. but i think at some point you have to decide whose version of you actually fits.
what if his version is what pushes me forward though
then keep it. just make sure it's a tool and not a sentence.
Long pause.
that's going in my notes app
go sleep. B+ means you showed up. that matters.

Ethan answered every single one carefully.

Like the messages mattered.

Because they did.

The clock crawled past midnight unnoticed.

Rain continued pouring outside.

And then—

At 1:13 AM, a new notification appeared.

Message from:

?
just.a.nobody00
0 followers  ·  no posts  ·  no profile picture

Ethan stared at the username for a second.

Blank account. No trace of a person. Just a question floating in from somewhere in the dark.

hey.
this is probably random but…
how do you stop feeling lonely even when you're surrounded by people all day?

Ethan read it once.

Then again.

Something about it sat differently from the others tonight.

Not the question itself — he'd gotten versions of it before.

It was the specificity. Surrounded by people all day. Like whoever wrote this wasn't describing loneliness in general.

They were describing their exact life.

His fingers hovered over the keyboard.

Outside, the rain kept falling.

— End of Chapter 1 —
